Our History at MotelPilot

Talk to our team
  1. Home
  2. Our History

Since 2016, we've helped independent owners share the daily workload without giving up ownership; here, we explain how that approach began.

Why we started in 2016

Our founders kept seeing independent owners trying to juggle pricing, online travel agencies and daily operations on their own.

So in 2016 we started MotelPilot in Canada: a remote service that takes on part of that work, and owners keep full ownership of their properties. We've been fully remote since day one. These days we handle management or revenue management for 60 hotels and motels.

The idea behind it hasn't changed: owners shouldn't have to do everything themselves.

Five steps from our first year to today

Our beginning (2016)

MotelPilot launched in Canada as a remote team helping independent owners with the day-to-day running of their properties.

Our first specialist clients (2017)

Our first standalone OTA and revenue management clients signed on, and we ran their Booking.com and Expedia listings as well as their pricing.

Reaching France and Australia (2020)

Independent owners in France and Australia started working with us, so our remote service now covered more time zones.

A 25-person team (2024)

There were 25 of us by then, working across hotel operations, revenue management and owner reporting.

60 hotels and motels today

We handle management or revenue management for 60 hotels and motels, for owners in Canada, the United States, France, Australia and other countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and why was MotelPilot started?

Our founders started MotelPilot in Canada in 2016 after seeing independent owners juggle pricing, online travel agencies and daily operations on their own. We built a remote service that takes on part of that work while owners keep full ownership of their properties.

Have you always worked remotely?

Yes, we've been fully remote since day one. Our idea hasn't changed: owners shouldn't have to do everything themselves.

Which countries do your clients come from?

We work for owners in Canada, the United States, France, Australia and other countries. Independent owners in France and Australia started working with us in 2020, so our remote service now covers more time zones.

How large are your team and client portfolio today?

By 2024, there were 25 of us, working across hotel operations, revenue management and owner reporting. Today we handle management or revenue management for 60 hotels and motels.

Tell us about your property

What would you like to take off your plate? Talk with our team about your property and let us know where you could use a hand.
