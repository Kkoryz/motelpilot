What happens after you request a quote

After you request a quote, you'll hear back within one business day.

From there, onboarding takes three steps: you send us your property details, you go over the work with us and set up access to your accounts and systems, and you finish the handover with our remote team before we get started.

What to include in your request

Property name and address

The name of your property and its full address, country included.

Room count

How many rooms or units you have.

Property type

Whether it's a motel, hotel, hostel, B&B, serviced apartments or short-term rentals.

Your systems

The property management system (PMS) and channel manager you use. If you don't use either, just say so.

Services you want

Remote day-to-day operations, OTA management, revenue management, or a mix of these.

Your main problem today

The issue you'd most like our help with.

Your questions before requesting a quote

Does the first call cost anything?

No. The 30-minute consultation is free and comes with no obligation. Using your property's own figures, we'll estimate the net effect before you commit to anything.

How long does a quote take?

Expect a reply within one business day, followed by a short call before we quote. How soon the written quote follows depends on the services you want, your room count and how many properties it covers, and we confirm that timing with you on the call. Hotels with more than 200 rooms, luxury properties and multi-property groups get a custom quote.

Do you need system access to quote?

To begin, we work from your property details and whatever figures you share on the call. Please don't put passwords in your request. If some access turns out to be needed, we'll talk it through with you before you set it up.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Asking for a quote doesn't tie you to a contract, and a standalone service needs no long-term contract. Your proposal shows each plan's minimum term before you agree anything, and after that term you can end the plan with 30 days' written notice and no exit fee.
