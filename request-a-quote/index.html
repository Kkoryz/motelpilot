Your questions before requesting a quote

Does the first call cost anything?

No. The 30-minute consultation is free and comes with no obligation. Using your property's own figures, we'll estimate the net effect before you commit to anything.

How long does a quote take?

Expect a reply within one business day, followed by a short call before we quote. Once we understand the work, we'll confirm the quote timeline with you. Hotels with more than 200 rooms, luxury properties and multi-property groups get a custom quote.

Do you need system access to quote?

To begin, we work from your property details and whatever figures you share on the call. Please don't put passwords in your request. If some access turns out to be needed, we'll talk it through with you before you set it up.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Asking for a quote doesn't tie you to a contract. You'll get the proposed engagement length and terms to look over first, and only then agree them directly with our team.
