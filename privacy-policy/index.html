MotelPilot Privacy Policy

We explain how we handle your personal data and how our responsibilities differ when processing guest information on a property's behalf.

Ask a privacy question
  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to personal data about people who visit our website or get in touch with us, property owners and their representatives, and guests whose data we handle under client agreements.

MotelPilot is a Canadian company, and we're responsible for the personal data we collect for our own purposes. Guest data we process on a property's behalf works differently: the property decides how it's used, and we act on its instructions as set out in our agreement.

At least once a year we review this policy, and we update it whenever our practices or legal obligations change.

The data we collect

Information you submit

Whatever you enter in our forms, such as your name, email address, phone number, property details and message.

Website visits

Technical and analytics information, including your IP address, browser, device, the pages you visit and the page that referred you to our site.

Client and guest records

Under client agreements, we handle owner contact details, booking records, guest contact information, stay details, guest communications and the operational reports needed to deliver the agreed services.

Card payments go only through PCI DSS-compliant processing partners, so card data never passes through our own systems.

How we use, retain and share data

Uses and legal grounds

Personal data lets us answer enquiries, deliver the services we've agreed to, keep in touch with you, see how people use our website and protect our systems.

Our legal basis depends on the activity and the law that applies. It can be consent, taking steps toward or performing a contract, a legal obligation, or legitimate interests that don't override your rights.

For guest data a client controls, we follow that client's documented instructions.

Retention

Data stays with us only as long as its purpose, our agreements and applicable legal requirements call for. We regularly go through what we hold and delete or anonymise anything we no longer need.

Client records are returned or deleted as the agreement sets out, unless the law says we must keep them.

Sharing

Some data has to go to the providers behind our forms, email, analytics and cloud hosting. Where a client agreement calls for it, data also goes to authorised service partners.

All of them must have appropriate confidentiality and data protection safeguards in place. We may also disclose data when the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

Your rights, cookies and data locations

Canada

Our handling of personal data follows PIPEDA. Within legal limits, you can ask to see or correct your data, withdraw consent and challenge how we handle it.

European Union

For hotels in France, we follow the GDPR. Where it applies, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority.

Australia

The Australian Privacy Act governs how we handle your information. Subject to its requirements, you can ask to access or correct your personal information and raise a privacy complaint.

United States

We comply with the privacy laws of the relevant US states.

Depending on your state and whether a given law applies, you may be able to access, correct or delete data, get a copy, appeal a decision or opt out of specified uses.

You won't be penalised for using any rights that apply to you.

Cookies and choices

Essential cookies keep the site working, and optional analytics cookies help us understand visits.

Where consent is required, optional cookies stay off until you agree. You can turn them down or withdraw consent in our cookie settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser.

Keep in mind that blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.

Storage and processing

Your data is hosted on cloud servers in Canada, and French properties can ask for EU storage. Our remote team and service providers may process data in other countries.

When required, we put appropriate transfer safeguards in place, including contractual protections. Storing data in the EU doesn't on its own mean all processing happens there.

Ask a privacy question or exercise your rights

You can use our contact page to send a privacy question or rights request. Let us know what you need and, if relevant, how you're connected to the property. Please leave payment card details and identity documents out of your first message.

You'll hear back from us within one business day. Before we complete a request, we may need to verify your identity or check with the property responsible for your data. Every request is handled within the time limits set by applicable law.
