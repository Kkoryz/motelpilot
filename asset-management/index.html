An Asset Management Company That Maximises Owner Value

Our job in motel asset management is to lift your motel's revenue and cost performance so the property earns you the highest possible yield.

Our asset management also takes on under-performing properties and turns their performance around. Day to day, we weigh operations against your financial targets.

We use market intelligence, structured analysis and industry practice to keep each decision in line with your objectives and to put your investment where it earns the best return.

Representing You With Brands and Operators

  • Owner profit first

    We judge every operator decision by what it does to your profit and cash flow, not by revenue or brand standards alone. This holds brands accountable and closes the trust gap between you and your operator.

  • Independent motel expertise

    If your motel or small hotel is unbranded or independently run, we work closely on its operations and supply the expertise your property lacks.

  • Your chosen team

    We represent you day to day with the operator you choose, including third-party operators, or with your own management team. We improve those relationships and agree practical fixes with them.

Using Your Approval Rights With Your Operator

As your appointed representative, we get our authority from the approval rights in your management or franchise agreement, not from your operator's goodwill. We use those rights to review proposals, and we settle challenges with evidence and contractual escalation.

Review, challenge and escalation under your existing agreement.

  1. Review and recommend

    As your appointed representative, we review operator budgets and capital plans, recommend whether to sign them off, and join owner meetings and monthly operations reviews by video. You keep final approval.

  2. Review operator pushback

    If the operator pushes back on our findings, we go through the data with them in a joint review within 10 business days.

  3. Recommend contractual action

    If the issue remains open after the joint review, we send you a written recommendation on using the performance and dispute terms in your agreement.

Properties of Every Size, Overseen Remotely

  • No size threshold

    Our asset management for motels and hotels takes on properties of any size. You don't need a minimum room count, revenue or asset value to work with us.

  • Smaller property scope

    For a smaller motel, we concentrate on quarterly property evaluations, an annual capital plan and monthly reviews of profitability against your financial goals.

  • Remote operational access

    We oversee operations, renovations and your operator remotely, using read access to your PMS and accounting systems, weekly reports from the operator and scheduled video walkthroughs.

  • Independent local inspections

    When a property needs an in-person inspection, we hire an independent local inspector or engineer and go through their report with you within 5 business days.

Initial Performance Review

  • Data and timing

    The review takes 4 to 6 weeks from kick-off to written recommendations. It starts once we have your last 24 months of P&Ls, your STR or benchmark reports, read-only access to your PMS and your management agreement.

  • Independent diagnostic work

    We carry out operational assessments and detailed benchmarking to find opportunities and give you detailed recommendations.

  • Commercial review scope

    This one-time review looks at market segmentation, positioning, strategy, the competitive landscape, market intelligence, revenue management strategy and marketing.

  • Operational review scope

    We go through departmental expenses, staffing, HR practices, operating processes and guest service to find where your motel's performance needs to improve.

  • Quantified action priorities

    Where your data supports an estimate, we put a number on the effect on revenue, GOP or cash flow. We then rank the recommendations in an 80/20 action plan by expected impact, urgency and the effort needed to implement them.

  • Your implementation plan

    Every recommendation comes with a priority, a responsible owner and a timeline. We take your team through it in a 60-minute walkthrough, and you or your operator can then implement the plan on your own.

  • Optional delivery support

    The review stands on its own. If you want help implementing it later, you can engage us for ongoing asset management, owner-side operator oversight or a specific follow-on project.

  • Written scope and fees

    You pay a one-time fixed fee for the review and for any follow-on project, and ongoing asset management is billed as a monthly retainer. We put the scope and the fees that apply in writing before any work starts.

Hands-On Ongoing Motel Asset Management Services

  • Strategy into action

    We put the strategic review into practice by repositioning and resetting your asset, budgeting revenue and improving market segmentation.

  • Commercial performance oversight

    We oversee your sales, marketing and revenue strategy, run ongoing operational and commercial reviews, and analyse your competitive set.

  • Operating efficiency improvements

    We optimise departmental expenses, staff allocation, internal processes, cash flow and working capital. We also review debtor management and purchasing and benchmark your cost KPIs.

  • Financial and capital controls

    We negotiate annual budgets, prepare P&L and cash flow forecasts, prepare and monitor CapEx budgets, and keep FF&E accounting and spending under control.

  • Annual budget scenarios

    We set your annual budget on the realistic case of a 12-month forecast run under conservative, realistic and optimistic scenarios, and use the other two cases to plan staffing, spending and pricing.

  • Clearly attributed results

    Every report compares your results with your competitive set, showing what came from market demand and what came from your motel outperforming it. We also separate the areas affected by capital work from the rest of the operation, which shows you the gains from a renovation.

  • Forward revenue intelligence

    Our revenue management reviews 12 months of pace every week and demand every business day. It also forecasts the current month and three and six months ahead, reviews intelligence every month and reviews rolling 12-month group rates every quarter.

  • Capital project delivery

    Our turnkey project management handles procurement, new builds, conversions, renovations, PIPs, building systems, 5-year budgeted capital and emergency capital, and coordinates the technical design and construction your contractors carry out, keeping disruption to your business to a minimum.

  • Reviewed financial reporting

    Through our accounting service, you get P&Ls reviewed by a staff accountant and a licensed CPA within 10 business days of month-end. You also get cash flow statements and prompt reconciliations of cash, assets and liabilities.

What Owner-Side Operator Oversight Includes

Choosing the right scope

Choose owner-side oversight when you trust your operator to implement our recommendations, because the results depend on your operator acting on them.

With full asset management, we deliver the plans ourselves and measure the RevPAR and GOP effect, though results aren't guaranteed.

Strategy and operating analysis

We review positioning, strategy, revenue budgets and forecasts, plan capital and investment, and evaluate internal processes and detailed internal data against industry benchmarks.

Owner investment advice

We advise you on franchise affiliation, management contract terms, budgets, capital investment and whether to buy, hold or sell, and we monitor how the asset is performing.

Limited owner time

Your part is a monthly report review and strategy call, plus any budget and capital decisions that need your approval.

Monthly operator accountability

Each month you get a report and a review meeting with your operator's operations team, covering revenue against budget and forecast, capital planning and open issues.

Budget and valuation reviews

Every year, we give you a written critique of the operator's proposed budget before you approve it. Every quarter, we update the estimate of your asset's value using NOI and cap rate trends.

Contract Renegotiation and Standalone Projects

  • Market terms comparison

    We compare your management contract or lease with current market terms and assess how suitable and effective your current operator is.

  • Commercial provision testing

    We test the base and incentive fees, performance clauses, term, termination rights and your obligations as owner, including reserves and funding calls.

  • Owner commercial representation

    We represent you when you renegotiate fees, performance clauses and contract term with your operator.

  • Your counsel's role

    We don't give legal advice. Your counsel checks the enforceability, liability and indemnity wording, then drafts and finalises the amendments that address our commercial findings.

  • Operator exit assessment

    If leaving is the better option, we assess your termination rights and exit costs and build your case from the performance-test results.

  • Planned operator transition

    If you choose to change operator, we plan a 90-day transition to the incoming operator.

  • Standalone project engagement

    You can commission a contract or lease review and renegotiation, or a buy, hold or sell analysis, without a management agreement.

Distressed Asset and Work-Out Support

Turnaround implementation

To put the plan into action, we audit sales and marketing, analyse the market and competitive set, reposition the asset, benchmark costs, work out CapEx needs and timing, and provide hands-on asset management.

Cash survival first

Within the first 10 business days of our remote diagnostic, we build a 13-week cash flow model. It shows the minimum trading cash you need for payroll, utilities, debt service and critical vendors.

Only after that do we move on to repositioning or a longer-term turnaround.

Written funding assessment

We tell you in writing whether recovery is feasible without more owner funding, or exactly how much extra funding is needed and when.

Independent recovery planning

To build a turnaround or work-out plan, we check business plans, carry out independent business reviews, review the finances and model future cash flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

What actual authority do you have over my operator or brand, and what happens when the operator pushes back on your findings?

Our authority as your appointed representative comes from the approval rights in your management or franchise agreement, not from your operator's goodwill. We use those rights when reviewing proposals, and when the operator pushes back, we resolve it with evidence and contractual escalation.

How many weeks does the performance review take from kick-off to recommendations, and which data do I need to provide?

From kick-off to written recommendations, the review takes 4 to 6 weeks. To start, we need your last 24 months of P&Ls, your STR or benchmark reports, read-only access to your PMS and your management agreement.

After the review, can I implement the plan myself, and can I buy the review on its own?

Yes. The review stands on its own for a one-time fixed fee, and every recommendation comes with a priority, a responsible owner and a timeline. After a 60-minute walkthrough, you or your operator can implement the plan yourselves, or engage us later for ongoing asset management, owner-side operator oversight or a specific follow-on project.

Can I engage you only for a single decision, such as renegotiating my management contract or a buy/hold/sell analysis, rather than ongoing representation?

Yes. A contract or lease review and renegotiation, or a buy, hold or sell analysis, can be commissioned as a standalone project without a management agreement. For a renegotiation, we test your fees, performance clauses, term and owner obligations and represent you with your operator, while your counsel drafts and finalises the amendments.

Before we commit to a turnaround, will you identify the minimum cash needed to keep trading and whether recovery is feasible without additional owner funding?

Yes. Within the first 10 business days of our remote diagnostic, we build a 13-week cash flow model showing the minimum trading cash you need for payroll, utilities, debt service and critical vendors. We then tell you in writing whether recovery is feasible without more owner funding, or exactly how much extra funding is needed and when.
