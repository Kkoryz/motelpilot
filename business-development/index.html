Business Development for Your Motel Portfolio

Our motel business development finds on- and off-market motel investment opportunities for you in the United States, Canada, France, Australia and the other countries where we work with owners.

We look at where value can be added through better operations, rebranding or renovation, and we give you lead-generation sources that keep new deals and motel business coming your way.

Off-Market Deal Sourcing and Due Diligence

  • Sourcing and screening

    Our network of hospitality owners brings us off-market targets, and we look at listings as well. We screen the market and its position in the cycle first, then the operations, using supply, demand, occupancy, ADR and RevPAR projections and competitive-set rates. You only see assets that pass both screens.

  • Remote document review

    We go through the P&Ls, rate and booking history, franchise and management agreements, vendor contracts and PIP documents. We check operations through scheduled video walkthroughs.

  • Independent site inspections

    Property condition, engineering, environmental and code inspections need professionals on site. We commission an independent local inspector or engineer, or work with the one your team appoints. We go through their report with you within 5 business days.

  • Acquisition diligence report

    For every acquisition we assess, you get a report covering local market trends, historical earnings and realistic earnings potential. It also includes a financial model projecting future results.

  • Obligations and competitive position

    Your diligence report looks at the management contract, franchise affiliation, competitive position, property improvement plan obligations and capital expenditure requirements.

  • Priced risks and decisions

    Every red flag comes with an estimated cost to fix it and our recommendation to either negotiate a lower price or walk away. The report ends with the acquisition risks, the unresolved issues and the conditions you need to meet before going ahead.

  • Pre-closing team review

    Before you close, our operations and sales teams remotely assess current operations, staffing, rate strategy and channel mix. They also review vendor, franchise and management contracts and your PIP and capital exposure.

  • Written asset review

    Within 10 business days of getting access to the seller's operating reports and data room, we send you the asset review report, which sets out prioritised risks and upside opportunities.

Who We Work With and How Mandates Work

  • Who we support

    Our business development for motels and hotels serves both first-time investors and portfolio owners, and acquisitions have no minimum deal size or room count.

  • Mandate arrangements

    Sourcing is non-exclusive by default. If you prefer, it can be exclusive for one market for 6 months. You choose the arrangement for your mandate.

  • Competing client interests

    When a deal suits several clients, we present it to them in the order they signed their mandates. We never advise two clients bidding on the same asset.

  • Your starting documents

    Send us your markets, room count, budget, target returns, proof of funds or a lender letter, your diligence team's contacts and a signed NDA. If you're a first-time investor, clear information lets us quickly find deals you can realistically close.

  • Transaction experience

    Since 2016, we've sourced, underwritten or supported 15 acquisitions and sales involving independent motels and hotels of 20 to 250 rooms. Before you sign, you can ask for an anonymised transaction list showing property types, sizes and our roles.

Underwriting and Investment Analysis

  • Investment assessment

    We look for upside in expanding your portfolio or repositioning. We underwrite each opportunity with market analysis, operating proformas and budgets.

  • Downside-led decisions

    We judge a deal by how well it holds up on the downside. Every model tests occupancy and ADR at 10% to 20% below the base case.

  • Cost and financing stress

    Every model also tests renovation costs 15% over budget, interest rates 200 basis points higher and exit cap rates 50 to 100 basis points wider.

  • Return and coverage effects

    For each downside case, you see its effect on IRR, cash-on-cash return and debt service coverage.

  • Working underwriting package

    Within 10 business days of receiving the offering materials and data, we deliver the complete underwriting, an Excel investment model with open formulas and a PDF investment summary.

  • Your advisers involved

    We work alongside your lender, broker and lawyers, and your team gets a working model to test its own assumptions.

  • Under-contract and refinancing support

    If a deal is already under contract, we take it on for underwriting and diligence only. We also support refinancing for motels and hotels you already own.

  • Focused assignment scope

    These assignments cover market performance trends, historical and achievable operating results, forecasts, and reviews of existing management and franchise agreements.

Debt and Equity Financing and Exit Strategy

Licensed partner delivery

We don't hold a real estate or mortgage broker licence. When placing debt or searching for buyers requires one, we direct licensed partner brokers in the relevant US state, Canadian province, France or Australia.

Debt and equity

We've helped investors raise both debt and equity. For acquisitions and development, the lenders we introduce you to include local and national banks, SBA lenders for US deals, CMBS, debt funds, bridge lenders and construction lenders.

We don't lend money ourselves.

Financing through closing

We put together your financing package and compare term sheets on total cost, covenants and flexibility, not just the headline rate. We support you through negotiations right up to closing, and our development support continues once you've chosen the motel.

Exit planning

We plan your exit at the same time as your financing. When you're ready to sell, we find buyers for the asset.

Brand Selection, Franchise and Repositioning

  • Brand selection

    We judge each flag by its net effect on your results, weighing local demand against fees, PIP requirements and contract terms. Then we negotiate your franchise agreement.

  • Product positioning

    We set your motel's positioning. When an existing property needs a new direction, we handle the repositioning and rebranding.

  • Conversion timing

    While your current franchise is still in force, we plan the conversion and negotiate the new agreement, and we time PIP delivery to match your exit date.

  • Legal responsibilities

    Your counsel handles termination notices, liquidated damages, settlements with the outgoing brand and the legal review of the new licence agreement.

  • Counsel coordination

    We give your legal team the brand timeline, PIP costs and rate data it needs for that work.

Design Review and Property Improvement Plans

Before construction starts, we review the design and architecture, and we price and manage your property improvement plan.

Your written review checks the layout, back-of-house areas, room mix, building systems and FF&E specifications against brand standards, operating flow and guest experience.

Every finding comes with a priority, a recommended change, an estimated cost and its operating impact, so you deal with the most consequential issues before you build.

You get the first report within 10 business days of us receiving the drawings, and we review revised sets within 5 business days.

Pre-Construction Team and FF&E Procurement

  • Project delivery and purchasing

    Our project management covers FF&E procurement and oversight of delivery. You get monthly reports and weekly updates. Any forecast more than 5% over a budget line needs approval. We complete takeover reviews within 10 business days of receiving the project documents.

  • Team and opening preparation

    Our motel management support coordinates the designers and consultants your project requires, franchisor and manager document checks, communication with the construction team, change-order and schedule decisions, and pre-opening budgets, procurement and marketing.

Construction Oversight and Site Reporting

We don't attend the property, so daily site supervision is handled by your general contractor's site superintendent or the owner's representative you appoint.

We need their detailed daily logs, photos and weekly video walkthroughs so we can review the work remotely, and we raise any issues with you within 3 business days.

Pre-Opening Services and Launch

  • Annual marketing plan

    Before you open, we prepare your motel's annual marketing plan, which sets the commercial plan for your launch.

  • Budget and revenue execution

    We prepare your operating budget before opening and put your revenue strategy into action from day one.

  • Motel staff recruitment

    We recruit and hire your motel's staff through our human resources service.

Deal Services Priced per Mandate

We price sourcing, underwriting, debt sourcing and buyer search for each mandate, and we agree the fees with you in writing before any work begins.

Fees are set by the size and complexity of your brief. Fixed work is on a retainer, work with uncertain results has a larger success component, and some briefs use a mix of the two. Success fees are paid when the deal completes.

Fixed Fees for Development Work

Before you sign, we quote development work based on its scope.

The quote is either a fixed project fee or a monthly retainer for the months the work runs, and it's never a percentage of your pre-opening or FF&E budgets.

Suppliers contract directly with your ownership entity at their own prices, so there's no resale and no mark-up, and we earn nothing from what you spend.

Free 30-Minute First Call

Your first consultation is a free 30-minute call with no obligation. On that call, we use your motel's figures to estimate the net effect before you commit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the smallest deal or key count you take on, and do you work with first-time motel investors as well as portfolio owners?

There's no minimum deal size or room count for acquisitions, and we support first-time investors as well as portfolio owners. If you're a first-time investor, clear information on your markets, budget and target returns lets us quickly find deals you can realistically close.

Which acquisition checks can your remote team complete, and which inspections must we commission locally?

Remotely, we go through the P&Ls, rate and booking history, franchise and management agreements, vendor contracts and PIP documents, and we check operations through scheduled video walkthroughs. Property condition, engineering, environmental and code inspections need professionals on site, so we commission an independent local inspector or engineer, or work with the one your team appoints, and go through their report with you within 5 business days.

Does your investment model show how lower demand, renovation overruns, higher borrowing costs and changing exit cap rates affect returns and debt coverage?

Yes. Every model tests occupancy and ADR at 10% to 20% below the base case, renovation costs 15% over budget, interest rates 200 basis points higher and exit cap rates 50 to 100 basis points wider. You see how each downside case changes your IRR, cash-on-cash return and debt service coverage.

Are sourcing, underwriting, debt sourcing and buyer search charged as a retainer, a success fee or a fixed fee per deal?

We price each mandate by the size and complexity of your brief and agree the fees with you in writing before any work begins. Fixed work is on a retainer, work with uncertain results has a larger success component, some briefs use a mix of the two, and success fees are paid when the deal completes.
