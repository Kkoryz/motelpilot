Key service agreement terms

  • Advance billing and payment

    Monthly fees are invoiced in advance at the start of each month, and payment is due within 15 days by ACH, international wire or credit card. Our prices are in USD, though you can pay in CAD, EUR or AUD at the exchange rate on the invoice date. A full management agreement also carries a one-time onboarding fee, separate from the revenue management setup fee.

  • Included and third-party costs

    Your fee pays for our team's time and our own pricing, reporting and BI tools. Third-party costs are billed to your property at cost, with no mark-up from us. These include your PMS, channel manager, other software subscriptions, OTA commissions, photographers and specialist partners.

  • Taxes and transfer charges

    USD prices don't include applicable sales taxes, and we add those only where the law requires it for your property's location. Your bank's transfer charges are yours to pay. Currency-conversion costs for payments in CAD, EUR or AUD are on us.

  • Ending a plan

    Each service's minimum term is set out in its proposal. Once that term is over, you can end your plan with 30 days' written notice, and there's no exit fee. The one-time setup fee can't be refunded after setup work starts, and we don't refund fees already paid for the current month.

  • Pausing a plan

    After the minimum term, you can also pause, again with 30 days' written notice. Monthly billing then stops for up to three months. You won't pay a pause fee, and if you restart within that time, you won't pay a new setup fee either.

  • Changing plans

    Upgrades take effect on the day you make the change, and the price difference is prorated for the rest of that month. Downgrades start from your next billing cycle.

  • Results and refunds

    Revenue results aren't guaranteed, and we don't offer refunds, because market demand, competitors and other outside factors all affect performance. What we do is work closely with you, check results against agreed targets on every monthly strategy call and change the strategy whenever a target is missed.
