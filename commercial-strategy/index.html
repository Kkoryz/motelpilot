One Motel Commercial Strategy for Every Property

Each of your properties gets its own motel commercial strategy and marketing plan.

That plan brings direct sales, global sales, eCommerce, revenue management, digital advertising and social media together under one strategy. We set shared objectives for your revenue, marketing, sales and operations teams so they work together to lift occupancy and go after demand on the dates that need business.

Our marketing team handles custom branding, account management, digital media and creative work.

The Team Behind Your Motel and Hotel Commercial Strategy

  • Planning and delivery

    We build your strategy with your leadership team from your data, market analysis and analytics, not from assumptions. Then we put it in place at your property and support it as it runs day to day.

  • International owner support

    Our team works with independent motel and hotel owners in many countries, including the United States, France, Canada and Australia.

Buying the Strategy on Its Own or Alongside Your Agencies

  • Standalone strategy

    You can buy a fixed-scope strategy on its own, without a management agreement or a revenue management plan, and add either one later.

  • Keep your agency

    Your current marketing agency or sales provider stays involved. We coordinate their work with your team under the commercial plan you've agreed.

  • Clear channel responsibilities

    We're responsible for strategy, revenue management, OTA and distribution management, and reporting. Your agency keeps the channels you choose for it, such as your website, SEO or advertising.

How Your Commercial Strategy Fee Is Quoted

Your fee is fixed before you sign.

It depends on which revenue, sales, marketing and creative pillars are included, how much advertising budget we manage, the creative workload, and the channels and properties covered.

Hotels with more than 200 rooms, luxury and full-service properties, and multi-property groups get an individual quote because their segmentation, channel mix and revenue exposure are more complex.

What the Fee Covers and What Costs Extra

Your revenue audit and strategy roadmap have one fixed project fee.

That fee includes the competitive-set and market data from our rate-shopping and market-share tools. You don't need to buy benchmark reports. If you already subscribe to them, we work yours into the plan and your monthly reporting. Team training costs extra.

Execution is commissioned separately, with its own fee for each service: website development, ongoing SEO, paid search campaigns, Google and metasearch setup, photoshoot management, OTA listing optimisation and new creative production.

References, Onboarding and What We Need From You

Onboarding lays the groundwork for delivery before any execution starts.

From you, we need your brand materials, one leadership contact, and your approval of the plan and of any major rate and budget changes. Before you sign, we arrange owner references and performance comparisons.

You can speak with up to 3 current owner clients who have agreed to take part. You also get anonymised RevPAR, occupancy and NOI figures that compare the 12 months before we took over with the 12 months after.

How the engagement starts.

  1. Study your property

    We study your property, its market and your goals.

  2. Agree scope and price

    We settle the scope and a fixed price with you.

  3. Arrange system access

    We take read-only access to your systems.

Planning Timeline and Plan Revisions

Allow four to eight weeks from our first meeting to a finished plan, signed off together in a final working session.

Plan-only buyers can add paid check-ins, and we revise the plan every six to eight weeks, or sooner if demand shifts. With execution, we adjust pricing each business day, review results against targets on a monthly strategy call and revise the full plan yearly with your budget.

Revenue Management, Pricing and Forecasting

  • Revenue strategy audit

    We audit your current revenue strategy, identify the changes it needs and make those changes ourselves, rather than leaving a list of recommendations with your team.

  • Systems and inventory

    We check that your revenue systems are set up and used correctly. Then we analyse your data and run inventory control and forecasting.

  • System compatibility

    Our revenue management works with Mews, Cloudbeds, Opera Cloud, RoomRaccoon and most other systems that support rate integration. If your system isn't on this list, send us its name and we'll confirm whether it works.

  • Investor forecasts

    Our asset forecasting uses your last 24 months of booking and rate history. It includes prediction intervals and is reviewed by our revenue team. You and your investors get a new forecast every week.

  • Outsourced channel management

    Our OTA management can be bought separately or as part of management. It covers Booking.com, Expedia and regional or niche channels, and we make sure your channel manager is mapped correctly.

Distribution, eCommerce and Paid Media

  • Paid advertising channels

    We run paid search, metasearch, social, video, display and native advertising to grow your direct bookings and make your brand more visible.

  • Booking and OTA advertising

    We run direct-booking advertising for your brand and sponsored placements on online travel agency sites. We also handle OTA marketing and technical support.

  • Search and local visibility

    We handle search engine optimisation and local search marketing. We also submit your property to GPS systems so travellers find you by location.

  • Budget allocation

    We manage all of this as one budget and move spend to the channels that bring you the most direct bookings for the money.

  • Hotel Ads delivery

    With our digital marketing, you pay by commission-per-stay or CPC, whichever fits your margins. Spend runs through your Google account, and we track conversions from launch. After 30 days, we check impressions, clicks, direct bookings and commission saved.

Global Sales and Group Business

Our sales team makes full use of your brand affiliation. We present your property to the largest travel agencies. If you don't have a sales team, our remote corporate and group sales service takes on that work, with no staff placed at your motel.

You name one on-site contact who shows the property, confirms space and welcomes arriving groups.

Performance Tracking and Commercial Reporting

  • Net channel revenue

    We report your revenue after OTA commissions, discounts and marketing spend. You see it next to your property's occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and gross revenue.

  • Campaign and sales returns

    We measure every sales initiative and paid campaign by what it actually returns, and we give you detailed performance reports and analytics.

  • Package and channel margins

    We work out the net profit on each package and the cost of sale for each channel. That shows you where extra volume eats into your margin.

Commercial Team Development, Processes and Technology

  • Coaching and operating procedures

    We coach your commercial team as they make live decisions. We also work with your revenue-generating departments to set best practices and standard operating procedures, so those practices carry on after we step back.

  • Commercial technology support

    Our technology service covers assessment, selection, installation and configuration. It also includes training your staff on your systems, marketing applications and programs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy the commercial strategy on its own, or only together with your management or revenue management service?

Yes, a fixed-scope strategy is available on its own with no management agreement or revenue management plan, and you can add either one later. Execution such as website development, ongoing SEO, paid search campaigns or OTA listing optimisation is commissioned separately, with its own fee for each service.

If I take the plan only versus plan plus execution, what do I get in each case and what is left for my team to do?

Plan only fits if you have an experienced commercial lead: your team acts on the written roadmap and makes the daily pricing, sales and marketing decisions, and check-ins, with plan revisions every six to eight weeks, are optional and cost extra. With plan plus execution, we handle your restrictions and distribution, adjust pricing each business day, run your campaigns each month, review results on a monthly strategy call and revise the full plan once a year alongside your budget.

How long does it take from our first meeting to a finished, agreed commercial plan?

It takes four to eight weeks, and the last step is a working session where we sign the plan off together. From you, we need your brand materials, one leadership contact and your approval of the plan and of any major rate and budget changes.

What makes the commercial strategy fee go up or down: which services I include, how much ad budget you manage, how much creative work, how many properties?

Your fee is fixed before you sign and depends on which revenue, sales, marketing and creative pillars are included, how much advertising budget we manage, the creative workload, and the channels and properties covered. Hotels with more than 200 rooms, luxury and full-service properties, and multi-property groups get an individual quote.
