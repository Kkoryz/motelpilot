Before you commit, we agree on the work and handover plan, and show you how our owner reporting works.

Start with a review of your property

We start by reviewing your property details and the work you want us to take on.

Together, we agree on the scope, account access and handover plan before work begins. Before you sign, we show you a redacted sample owner reporting pack and give you a live 30-minute demo of our reporting platform.

Both are covered by a mutual non-disclosure agreement that protects your figures and our other owners’ data.

What we need from you

Property details

We need your property’s address, lodging type, number of rooms or units, and the services you want us to handle.

Listing links

Send us your current listing links, including Booking.com and Expedia where applicable, so we can review your channels.

Read-only access first

For the initial review, give us read-only access to your pricing tool or channel manager. We never need payment credentials or full account control.

  • Access for the agreed work

    For pricing work, we need permission to adjust rates in the relevant tool. For OTA work, we need user access to the relevant extranets and channel manager. For remote day-to-day operations, we also need PMS user access and copies of current vendor contracts to review, keep or renegotiate. You can revoke our access once the work is done.

  • Your decision-maker

    Name the owner or authorised contact who will approve the scope, pricing boundaries and vendor changes. We need their contact details and a backup contact for time-sensitive decisions.

Your three onboarding steps

  1. 01

    Send us your property details

    You send the address, listing links and a summary of what you need. Allow around 15–30 minutes to gather these; we reply within one business day.

  2. 02

    Agree the work and set up access

    We review the scope together and show you our reporting platform. You nominate an approver, arrange the agreed user access and supply relevant vendor contracts. Allow roughly 1–3 business days, depending on access readiness; the reporting demo takes 30 minutes.

  3. 03

    Complete the handover so we can begin

    Our remote handover team coordinates the transition so sales and operations keep running. You provide current procedures, local contact details and outstanding priorities. We connect to your existing systems and review each channel, keeping your accounts, PMS history and future reservations under your name. Most quick services finish within 2 to 3 business days after we receive details and access; PriceLabs work typically takes 1–2 business days. For a full hotel handover, we establish operations and marketing workflows within the first 30 days, with multi-property groups delivered in batches. Our team works entirely remotely; partners or local contractors carry out on-site work.
