    Our Process for Getting Started with MotelPilot

    You know exactly what we will do before any work starts.

    Start with a call
    1. Home
    2. Our Process
    • 2 to 3 business days Quick services turnaround
    • 1–3 business days Scope and access setup
    • 1–2 business days PriceLabs work
    • 30 minutes Reporting demo length

    Start with a review of your motel

    Our process starts with a review of your motel's details and the work you want us to take on.

    Together, we agree on the scope, account access and handover plan before work begins. Before you sign, we show you a redacted sample owner reporting pack and give you a live 30-minute demo of our reporting platform.

    Both are covered by a mutual non-disclosure agreement that protects your figures and our other owners’ data.

    What we need from you

    Property details

    We need your property’s address, lodging type, number of rooms or units, and the services you want us to handle.

    Listing links

    Send us your current listing links, including Booking.com and Expedia where applicable, so we can review your channels.

    Read-only access first

    For the initial review, give us read-only access to your pricing tool or channel manager.

    Revenue and OTA work never needs payment credentials or full account control; accounting and project payments use limited payment rights in your own bank account, with your approval.

    Access for the agreed work

    For pricing work, we need permission to adjust rates in the relevant tool. For OTA work, we need user access to the relevant extranets and channel manager.

    For remote day-to-day operations, we also need PMS user access and copies of current vendor contracts to review, keep or renegotiate. You can revoke our access once the work is done.

    Your decision-maker

    Name the owner or authorised contact who will approve the scope, pricing boundaries and vendor changes. We need their contact details and a backup contact for time-sensitive decisions.

    Your three onboarding steps

    1. Send us your property details

      You send the address, listing links and a summary of what you need. Allow around 15–30 minutes to gather these; we reply within one business day.

    2. Agree the work and set up access

      We review the scope together and show you our reporting platform. You nominate an approver, arrange the agreed user access and supply relevant vendor contracts. Allow roughly 1–3 business days, depending on access readiness; the reporting demo takes 30 minutes.

    3. Complete the handover so we can begin

      Our remote handover team coordinates the transition so sales and operations keep running. You provide current procedures, local contact details and outstanding priorities. We connect to your existing systems and review each channel, keeping your accounts, PMS history and future reservations under your name. Most quick services finish within 2 to 3 business days after we receive details and access; PriceLabs work typically takes 1–2 business days. For a full motel handover, we establish operations and marketing workflows within the first 30 days, with multi-property groups delivered in batches. Our team works entirely remotely; partners or local contractors carry out on-site work.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What access will you need to my systems?

    For the initial review, read-only access to your pricing tool or channel manager is enough, and revenue and OTA work never needs payment credentials or full account control. For the agreed work, we need permission to adjust rates for pricing, user access to your extranets and channel manager for OTA work, and PMS user access plus current vendor contracts for remote day-to-day operations. Once the work is done, you can revoke our access.

    How much of my time does onboarding take?

    Gathering your property details takes around 15–30 minutes. Agreeing the work and setting up access takes roughly 1–3 business days, depending on how ready your access is, and the reporting demo takes 30 minutes.

    How long do quick services and a full motel or hotel handover take?

    Most quick services finish within 2 to 3 business days after we receive your details and access, and PriceLabs work typically takes 1–2 business days. For a full motel handover, we establish operations and marketing workflows within the first 30 days, and multi-property groups are delivered in batches.

    Can I see your owner reporting before I sign?

    Yes. We show you a redacted sample owner reporting pack and give you a live 30-minute demo of our reporting, both covered by a mutual non-disclosure agreement that protects your figures and our other owners' data.

    Talk through your next steps

    Start with a call to discuss your motel, the support you need and your preferred timing. Contact us to arrange a call; we reply to every enquiry within one business day.
