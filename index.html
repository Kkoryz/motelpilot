Answers before you get started

Which properties do you take on?

Motels, hotels, hostels, B&Bs, serviced apartments, short-term rentals and vacation rental portfolios. There's no minimum number of units.

Is there a long contract?

Not for a standalone service, which needs no long-term contract. For any plan, your proposal sets out the minimum term, and once it's over you can end or pause the plan with 30 days' written notice and no exit fee.

How are fees set?

Full management is priced as a share of your total revenue or as a fixed monthly fee set by your room count, and revenue management plans as a fixed monthly fee for your room band. After a first call, you get a written proposal with the fees for your property before you commit.

Who handles on-site work?

Anything that needs someone at the property is done by our partners or local contractors. Our own team is remote only.

How does the handover work?

First we go over the agreed work with you, set up account and system access, and confirm your leadership contact. Access starts out read-only, and the plan and any major rate or budget changes need your sign-off.

Talk through your property's needs

Tell us about your property and what you'd like to hand over. You can arrange a free 30-minute consultation with our team with no obligation, and you'll hear back within one business day.
