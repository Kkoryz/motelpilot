Outsourced revenue management company working remotely for your property

We run the day-to-day revenue management for your hotel or resort remotely. That covers pricing, forecasting, distribution and ad-hoc group quotations. We tailor the service to your property and scale it as your needs grow. You can choose one of three plans or a customised offer.

Three plans for how much you want to hand off

We build our plans around how much strategic scope and direct contact you want. They don't differ in how often daily pricing happens.

  • Base

    Strategic scope and contact
    One strategy call a month, and you can book extra meetings.

  • Extended

    Strategic scope and contact
    Two strategy calls a month, and you can book extra meetings. You also get OTA management and metasearch optimisation on Booking.com, Expedia and Google, plus promotions strategy, competitor tracking and segmentation across OTA, direct and corporate business.

  • Complete

    Strategic scope and contact
    Includes OTA management. On top of that, you get near-daily active management, a weekly video strategy meeting with your dedicated strategist and a quarterly business review.

Dynamic pricing connected to the PMS you already use

Before you sign, we check that your PMS supports two-way rate integration. That way, prices reach your channels without anyone re-entering them by hand. We work with Mews, Cloudbeds, Opera Cloud, RoomRaccoon and most other systems that support rate integration.

If your system isn't on that list, send us its name and we'll confirm whether it qualifies.

We set and adjust your room rates every day

  • Daily pricing decisions

    Your dedicated revenue manager sets your room rates every day. They also make your pricing, channel and inventory decisions daily, based on booking pace, pickup, demand, seasonality and your competitive set.

  • Approved price boundaries

    Before we change any prices, we agree with you on a fixed minimum for each room type and a ceiling for peak demand. We then build those limits into your pricing rules.

  • Daily movement caps

    You can also cap how much your rates move in a single day, for example at 15%. We only price within the limits you've approved, and you can change them whenever you like.

  • Continuous market checks

    Every day, we compare your rates with up to 10 similar properties nearby. We review your pricing and calendars every 24 hours and track your market and competitors in real time.

  • Your local hours

    Your named manager responds during your local hours. We schedule sessions and reviews in your property's time zone, whether you're in Australia, Europe or North America.

  • Uninterrupted coverage

    We monitor your rates on weekends and public holidays. When your manager is away, another revenue manager who already knows your property covers for them.

  • Limited property portfolios

    Each dedicated revenue manager looks after no more than 12 properties at a time, so your hotel gets attention every day.

Demand forecasts that become budgets and targets

We turn your demand forecasts into rooms revenue budgets and business targets, and we revalidate them on a rolling basis.

You get owner-ready demand forecasts, booking pace reports and annual revenue budgets. Your forecasts are validated for the current month plus three months and for the current month plus six months. On the Complete plan, we also convert the forecast into a 12-month pricing calendar.

Forecast-led budgeting, revalidated on a rolling basis.

  1. Combine historical and market data

    Combine your booking history and past performance with market trends.

  2. Forecast demand

    Project future occupancy and revenue.

  3. Build budgets and targets

    Build your rooms revenue budget and business targets from the forecast.

  4. Revalidate on a rolling basis

    Revalidate the forecast rather than fixing it once a year.

Channel mix, parity and distribution across every OTA

  • Connected channel distribution

    We set your prices and availability on OTAs and on your direct channel, and we map your rooms and rates in your channel manager. We also check parity across every channel, which reduces overbooking.

  • Protected direct value

    We structure your channel rate plans, weighting and differentials to build visibility and protect your average rates. This prevents cannibalisation, ranking damage and prices below your direct rate, and it keeps direct booking the best value.

  • Commission and contract support

    Our OTA optimisation includes negotiating your Booking.com and Expedia commissions using your booking volume. Your contracts stay in your name, and any change needs your approval.

More revenue management services for your property

  • Hotel Ads management

    Through our digital marketing, we manage your Hotel Ads bids and budgets on commission-per-stay or CPC terms. We track conversions and review the results after 30 days.

  • Group purchasing access

    Our group purchasing covers the United States, Canada, France and Australia, and revenue-only clients can use it too. Orders are placed in your name, and we pass the negotiated savings on to you in full.

  • Free initial consultation

    Our free consultation is a 30-minute call with no obligation. We use your hotel's figures to estimate the net effect before you commit.

  • Owner references and results

    Before you sign, we give you references and results. That means calls with up to 3 owners, plus anonymised RevPAR, occupancy and NOI that compare the 12 months before our management with the 12 months after.

  • Quick service delivery

    With our quick-service process, we complete most quick services within 2 to 3 business days of receiving your property details and access.

  • PriceLabs service timing

    Our PriceLabs process takes 1 to 2 business days once we have your property details and access.

Group, corporate and tour operator pricing

We price your group, corporate, event and tour operator business against the forecast for the relevant dates, not with fixed year-round discounts.

We also set availability strategies for your corporate and tour operator demand. Through our hotel management support, we co-lead your RFP pricing and submissions so you meet deadlines. We also help you follow up on unsuccessful bids, and we develop destination-themed packages.

Reports and benchmarks you can act on

  • Weekly performance visibility

    Every week we send you a summary of your occupancy, ADR and RevPAR. Between reports, you can check an online dashboard that we refresh every 24 hours.

  • Monthly reporting and accountability

    Each month you get a report on your own property covering visibility, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and revenue. It comes with an activity log that records every action our team has taken.

  • Direct urgent support

    You can reach your revenue manager by email, phone or shared messaging. When you send us an urgent request, such as closing dates or changing rates, we act on it within 2 hours.

Pricing strategy setup, from market study to first rate

Before we change any of your prices, we complete a one-time pricing strategy setup. It takes us from assessing your property and connecting your systems through to daily management.

Alongside the setup checks, we analyse your revenue potential and market positioning, and we review the processes you already have.

  1. Sign the agreement

    The one-time setup begins after you sign.

  2. Complete pricing setup

    Over 5 to 10 business days, we review your market, room types, published rates, rate history and systems, connect to your PMS or channel manager and build pricing rules.

  3. Begin daily pricing

    We start pricing your hotel every day as soon as setup is complete.

Your RMS and channel manager stay in place

You keep your RMS, PMS and channel manager.

We run your pricing and OTA operations together on the setup you already have. Before we change any prices, we review your RMS rules, channel mappings and rate plans. Then we agree with you which system controls each room type and rate plan.

For rates your RMS controls, your RMS stays the only tool that pushes rates, and we set the strategy, rules, floors and ceilings.

For rates that are priced manually, you turn off automatic RMS updates for those rates, and we log every override and give it an end date. Every 24 hours we compare your RMS with your channel manager, so manual changes and automation never overwrite each other.

The access we need, and what stays yours

We only ask for the narrowest access the work needs.

For setup and audits, read-only access is enough. For daily management, we need your listing links. Depending on your systems, we also need either a dedicated named OTA user or rate-level permissions in your PMS or channel manager that cover only rates, availability, restrictions and listing content.

We never ask for payment credentials or full admin control to manage your revenue. Your accounts stay in your name, and you can withdraw our access whenever you choose.

Revenue tools included, third-party software at cost

  • Included revenue tools

    The revenue management, rate shopping and business intelligence tools we supply from our own stack are included in your monthly plan fee.

  • Ongoing system maintenance

    We support the ongoing maintenance of the systems we provide, and we use our own tools wherever they fit your property's needs.

  • Third-party software costs

    Keep any third-party tools that serve a specific need at your property. PriceLabs, Wheelhouse and other subscriptions are billed to you separately at cost, and we don't add a mark-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you change rates on your own authority, or do I approve changes first — and can I set limits such as floor rates or a maximum move per day?

Before we change any prices, we agree with you a fixed minimum for each room type and a ceiling for peak demand, and build those limits into your pricing rules. You can also cap how much rates move in a single day, for example at 15%. Your revenue manager then sets rates daily only within the limits you've approved, and you can change them whenever you like.

For daily rate changes, what access do you need to my PMS, channel manager and OTA extranets — read-only, a dedicated user, or full extranet login?

Read-only access is enough for setup and audits. For daily management, we need your listing links and, depending on your systems, either a dedicated named OTA user or rate-level permissions in your PMS or channel manager that cover only rates, availability, restrictions and listing content. We never ask for payment credentials or full admin control, and you can withdraw our access whenever you choose.

Which RMS, rate shopping and BI tools do you provide in the package, and are their subscription costs included in the monthly plan fee or billed separately?

The revenue management, rate shopping and business intelligence tools we supply are included in your monthly plan fee. Third-party tools you keep for a specific need, such as PriceLabs or Wheelhouse, are billed to you separately at cost, with no mark-up.

No. Each plan covers one property, and you pay a fixed monthly fee for your room band, not a per-room price or a share of your hotel's revenue.
