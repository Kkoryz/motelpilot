Careers at MotelPilot

Ask about joining us
  1. Home
  2. Careers

As part of our remote team, you'll help independent owners run their properties through daily operations, room pricing and reporting.

Your work on our remote team

There are 25 of us, and we look after day-to-day operations for independent hotel and motel owners in countries such as the United States, France, Canada and Australia.

You'd help run those operations, manage Booking.com and Expedia listings, set room rates and prepare owner reports. All of it is remote. Shared task lists, written handovers and regular team calls keep things moving across time zones.

If a job needs someone at the property, we arrange it with partners or local contractors instead of sending our own staff.

Why join our team

Remote work

The whole team is remote. We agree on coverage hours, so you always know when your teammates are around.

English and French

Both languages come up in our daily work, so if you're bilingual, you'll get to use both.

Learning across markets

Lessons from different properties and countries get shared around the team. That shows you how local demand and guest needs shape decisions.

Room to grow

Peer coaching is part of how we work. Over time you can take on more properties, train colleagues or lead a small team.

Open roles

  • Remote Hotel Operations Coordinator

    Americas time zones · Full time · English and French. In this role you'd handle daily property tasks, keep handovers clear and work with local partners on on-site requests.

  • Revenue Analyst

    European time zones · Full time · English required; French helpful. This job means reviewing demand, adjusting room rates and availability, and explaining in owner reports why performance changed.

  • OTA Distribution Specialist

    Asia-Pacific time zones · Part time · English required; French helpful. You'd look after Booking.com and Expedia listings, check that rates and inventory are accurate, and sort out distribution issues.

How to apply

Send your application through our contact page. Tell us which role you want, add a link to your CV, and give your time zone, availability and the languages you work in. A short note on your relevant experience and what draws you to the role helps too.

If your application goes ahead, there are two remote interview rounds. The first is a conversation about your experience and availability, and the second is a practical discussion with the team you'd be joining.
