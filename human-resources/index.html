Hospitality Human Resources Built Around Your Leadership

Pay scales, yearly wage rises, bonuses and dismissals stay under your final control.

Our motel human resources team works directly with your senior leadership on people strategy and performance management. Inside the budget you approve, we set your employees' hourly rates and propose any disciplinary measures. Any salary or senior appointment outside that budget needs your approval before it goes ahead.

Nobody is terminated until you or the manager you nominate signs off.

Getting Started: Property Review & Handover

The handover, the property review and our staffing recommendations set the priorities for your people function. Once we receive your complete set of documents, your people function is fully running within 30 days.

  1. Complete document handover

    Send your organisation chart, employee files and contracts, the last 12 months of payroll registers, handbook and policies, open vacancies and any collective agreement in one batch. Partial setup does not start on incomplete files.

  2. Detailed property review

    Review the property, operations, staffing, market position and financials to identify opportunities for improvement and growth.

  3. Staffing priorities and recommendations

    Use payroll and time records, labour cost per occupied room, overtime, turnover, occupancy forecasts and guest review scores to set staffing priorities and recommend changes.

Who We Are & Which Properties Qualify

  • Our company

    We provide human resources services for motels and hotels, either on their own or as part of full management.

  • Any property size

    There's no minimum headcount for you to qualify. A 20-room motel with five staff is eligible for our HR service.

  • Small-team scope

    For a small property like yours, we concentrate on recruitment, onboarding, payroll and employment compliance, and we leave out programmes designed only for larger hotels.

  • Our wider team

    Our team is 25 people working across motel and hotel operations, revenue management and owner reporting, and they all support your property.

Remote Hiring With On-Site Support

We recruit, place and onboard your candidates remotely, and we interview them by video.

Your ownership entity stays the legal employer. We draft the offer letters and employment contracts for you to sign, and we send them out with the onboarding forms for electronic signature. You choose a named general manager, on-site manager or local contact.

That person checks original identity and right-to-work documents in person and runs any in-person final interviews or trials you ask for. On day one, they also hand out uniforms and keys and give a tour of the property. We handle every other step remotely.

Your Named HR Contact, Ready to Help

You get one named HR contact for your motel, not a shared ticket queue. Your managers phone or email that person directly with day-to-day HR questions, and they get an answer within one business day.

Recruitment & Team Building

  • Hiring decisions and timing

    Before we start searching, we agree your budget and how you want sign-off to work. Within that budget, we hire with your general manager, unless you want to approve hires yourself. Once the search starts, hourly roles take 14 to 21 days and management roles take 30 to 45 days.

  • Candidate and contract fit

    We screen candidates for the skills each role needs and for a personality that suits your motel. We recruit hourly, salaried and tipped employees on full-time, part-time, seasonal and temporary terms.

  • Departments and seasonal preparation

    We recruit across front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, food and beverage, and management. If your property is seasonal, we start 8 weeks before opening, so your team is ready on day one.

  • Training and development

    Your front-desk team completes six structured remote video sessions within the first 30 days of working with us. We also develop and support staff retention, deliver leadership development, and provide sales and marketing training and direction.

  • Replacement recruitment

    If someone we recruited resigns or is dismissed for performance within their first 90 days, we find you a replacement with no additional recruitment fee.

  • Required pre-start checks

    Before anyone starts, we check at least two employment references, the required licences and certificates (including food-handling or first-aid credentials), and their right to work under your country’s rules. All of these checks are finished within 5 business days of the offer.

  • Conditional background screening

    Where local law permits it and your candidate consents, we arrange background checks through licensed local screening providers, and your property is billed at cost.

  • Full motel management

    With third-party management, we run an independent motel’s daily operations remotely while you keep ownership. We recruit, onboard, orient and train your General Manager and Director of Sales, including brand immersion when it’s required.

Full Range of HR Functions, Options & Pricing

  • People programmes

    Our HR lead oversees organisational design, compensation and benefits, talent acquisition and retention, cultural immersion, onboarding, training, change management, safety, risk control, and employee and labour relations.

  • HR administration

    We administer your payroll, benefits, policies, procedures and compliance, manage your HR technology and reporting, and provide consulting for your motel.

  • Scope you choose

    You buy only the modules you need. Full HR planning, executive search, recruitment, payroll, and consulting and policy projects are each available on their own. Ongoing leadership of your property team requires full management.

  • Existing employment arrangements

    We take on your current staff, contracts, handbook and policies as they stand, and we propose changes only after we’ve reviewed them with you.

  • Unionised properties

    We keep the wage, scheduling and grievance terms of your existing collective agreement, and we hold our first video meeting with union representatives within 7 days of starting.

  • Management fee coverage

    Under full management, your management fee covers staff leadership, recruitment and payroll processing for your team.

  • Standalone service charges

    For standalone recruitment, you pay per hire; for HR consulting or policy work, you pay a fixed project fee.

  • Your advance quote

    We give you a quote before you sign, based on your headcount, the number of hires you expect each year, the modules you select and how many properties you have.

Staffing, Payroll, Reporting & Incident Response

  • Staffing and shift schedules

    We prepare departmental staffing tables, schedules, screening steps, and hiring and training guidelines, and we adjust staffing every week to match forecast occupancy. Your on-site manager publishes the rota and handles same-day swaps.

  • Your system choice

    We set up one cloud HR and payroll system for your payroll and employee records. If your existing HRIS, payroll provider and time-tracking system give us remote user access, we keep them instead.

  • Monthly owner reporting

    Each month, your owner reporting pack covers headcount, turnover, labour cost against budget, open positions, training completion and the incident log.

  • Quarterly workforce review

    Every quarter, we add a workforce review that sets out trends and our staffing recommendations for your property.

  • Immediate on-site response

    We don’t have any staff at your property. Your on-site manager or local contact handles immediate safety and calls emergency services first, then contacts your named HR contact.

  • Urgent HR issues

    Your managers report workplace injuries, harassment complaints, no-shows and sudden resignations to your named HR contact, and we reply within one business day.

  • Incident records and investigation

    We guide your incident records and your insurance or workers’ compensation filings, start harassment investigations within one business day, and help your team arrange shift cover remotely.

  • Payroll across roles

    Our payroll service covers tipped, hourly and salaried employees, distributes service charges, and pays staff who work across departments at the correct rate for each role.

Labor Law Compliance

  • Pre-takeover compliance review

    Before we take over, we go through your contracts, handbook, pay, overtime, timekeeping and required workplace notices to find any compliance gaps.

  • Written findings and fixes

    Within 15 business days of receiving your documents, you get our written findings with prioritised fixes, and we deal with the most serious gaps first.

  • Employment law monitoring

    We keep track of employment law changes in your jurisdiction, including minimum wage increases, health care rules and overtime requirements.

  • Updates before implementation

    When the law changes, we update the affected policies, pay rates and processes before the change takes effect, and we list every update in your monthly report.

  • Local jurisdiction coverage

    We cover US states, Canadian provinces, France, Australia and the other countries where we work with owners. We check your policies against the rules in your jurisdiction and confirm the requirements with local employment counsel.

  • Accountable legal review

    Our HR lead oversees every compliance check, and counsel licensed in your country, state or province reviews each new policy before it goes live.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hire you just for HR or recruitment without handing over management of the motel, and what scope options do you offer?

Yes, we provide HR services on their own or as part of full management, and you buy only the modules you need. Full HR planning, executive search, recruitment, payroll, and consulting and policy projects are each available on their own; only ongoing leadership of your property team requires full management.

Who remains the legal employer, and which hiring, pay and dismissal decisions remain with the owner?

Your ownership entity stays the legal employer, and pay scales, yearly wage rises, bonuses and dismissals stay under your final control. Within the budget you approve, we set hourly rates and propose disciplinary measures, but any salary or senior appointment outside that budget needs your approval, and nobody is terminated until you or the manager you nominate signs off.

How do you recruit for my motel remotely, how long does it take to fill a role, and do I approve each hire?

We recruit, place and onboard candidates remotely and interview them by video, while your named local contact checks original identity and right-to-work documents in person. From the start of the search, hourly roles take 14 to 21 days to fill and management roles take 30 to 45 days. Within the agreed budget, we hire with your general manager, unless you want to approve hires yourself.

How is HR charged — per employee, per hire, per property — and is it included in the management fee?

If we manage your motel, staff leadership, recruitment and payroll processing are covered by your management fee. For standalone recruitment you pay per hire, and for HR consulting or policy work you pay a fixed project fee. We quote before you sign, based on your headcount, the hires you expect each year, the modules you select and how many properties you have.

Is your HR service worth it for a 20-room motel with five staff, or is it for larger hotels?

There's no minimum headcount, so a 20-room motel with five staff is eligible. For a small property, we concentrate on recruitment, onboarding, payroll and employment compliance, and leave out programmes designed only for larger hotels.
