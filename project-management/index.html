MotelPilot's Hotel Project Management Services

We manage your hotel project throughout development and act as the go-between for you and your brand, covering design, construction management and technical services.

From you, we need site and title documents, existing surveys, drawings and brand correspondence. We also need one decision-maker who approves budget, design and contractor choices within 5 business days.

Your time commitment is about 2 to 4 hours a week for a 30-minute weekly video meeting and sign-offs. In the 3 months before opening, it rises to around 6 hours a week.

From Feasibility Study to Opening Day

With our development services, you have one accountable partner from feasibility through opening, including approvals and pre-opening support.

We quote you before you sign, either as a scope-based fixed fee or as a monthly retainer, not as a percentage of your budget. We take no resale, mark-up or supplier-spend commission.

When long-lead equipment is delayed, we use brand-approved substitutions and fallback arrangements to keep your opening on schedule.

From identifying long-lead items to arranging opening-day alternatives.

  1. Flag long-lead items

    Flag long-lead FF&E items when the order is placed.

  2. Track against opening

    Track each item against the opening date every week.

  3. Submit substitutes

    If an item is late or unavailable, propose pre-specified substitutes and submit them for brand approval within 5 business days.

  4. Arrange fallback items

    Where approval or delivery still cannot meet the date, arrange rental or in-stock items so the opening goes ahead on schedule.

New Builds, Conversions & Renovations We Manage

  • Turnkey capital projects

    We oversee your capital improvement programme, including capital management, contractor selection, design oversight, contract administration, construction coordination and quality control.

  • Builds and conversions

    We manage ground-up new builds and major conversions, and we coordinate construction so it meets brand standards across the whole project.

  • Renovations and repositioning

    We handle franchise-mandated PIPs, lifecycle renovations, boutique repositionings, resort renovations, interior upgrades and building-system replacements. When your hotel is operating, we phase the work so it keeps earning.

  • Planned and emergency capital

    We manage capex projects, 5-year budgeted capital and emergency capital. Technical design and construction services are included in our scope.

  • Property sizes

    No project is too small for us. A 40-room motel renovation fits within our range. Most of our projects are for motels, economy and select-service hotels, boutiques and resorts of 10 to 200 rooms. Larger hotels get a custom scope.

  • Hotel technology projects

    Our hotel IT project management is a turnkey service for your technology projects, including those that are part of a new development.

Project Timelines, Budgets & Monthly Cost Reporting

  • PIP timeline

    What you receive
    4 to 8 months from appointment to closeout. About 2 to 3 months of that is pre-construction.

  • Full renovation timeline

    What you receive
    9 to 15 months, of which 3 to 5 months is pre-construction.

  • New-build timeline

    What you receive
    18 to 30 months, of which 6 to 12 months is pre-construction.

  • Pre-construction

    What you receive
    These periods include design, permits, pricing and procurement.

  • Total project budget

    What you receive
    Your budget covers hard construction costs; architect, engineer and consultant fees; permits and connections; FF&E, OS&E and IT; pre-opening costs; and construction-period financing.

  • Contingency

    What you receive
    We set aside 10% of hard costs for new builds and 15% for renovations at operating hotels. No draw happens without your prior written approval.

  • Budget and schedule

    What you receive
    We give you both at appointment and track performance against them throughout. When a budget line is forecast to exceed its allowance by more than 5%, we flag it for your approval.

  • Monthly reporting

    What you receive
    You get a written cost report, a dashboard and a monthly owner report. Together they show approved budgets, signed-contract and purchase-order commitments, actual spending, forecast cost to complete and the funding still required. You can also view the dashboard live.

  • Cost accountability

    What you receive
    We track committed, forecast and contingency costs by budget line, programme and property, and we link every cost change to the decision behind it.

  • Progress reporting

    What you receive
    Our reports cover schedule progress, critical-path milestones, open risks and every approved or pending scope change. Between monthly reports, we send you a short weekly progress email.

  • Closeout documents

    What you receive
    You receive PDFs of the as-built drawings, operation and maintenance manuals, warranties, guarantees, contractor final releases, final cost reconciliation and the completed punch list.

Contracts, Payments, Inspections & Project Takeovers

  • Your existing team

    You keep your architect, interior designer, general contractor and consultants, along with your existing contracts, supplier agreements and signed purchase orders. We recommend new firms only for roles that aren't filled.

  • Takeover review

    If design or construction has already started, we review your budget, schedule, open contracts, open change orders and every commitment within 10 business days of receiving your project documents.

  • Written recovery plan

    You get a takeover report that sets out the gaps, where the project stands now and the recovery actions. It includes a keep, renegotiate or rebid recommendation for each commitment. Nothing changes until you approve it.

  • Remote inspection review

    We don't attend your property. Your architect, your contractor or an independent inspector you appoint inspects and signs off completed work. Within 3 business days, we review their reports, photos and video against your operating plans and brand standards, then send you any corrections.

  • Credentials and insurance

    Our project managers hold PMP certification and have construction project experience. We carry professional liability insurance for this work, and we send you the certificate before you sign.

  • Owner agency

    We act as your agent, not your builder. You contract directly with contractors and vendors. Where you need it, we review bids and select or recommend firms.

  • Payment controls

    We handle draw requests, payments and lien waivers. We check each draw against its contract, the completed-work sign-off and the waivers. We release payment from your account only after you give final approval.

  • Cost and delivery trade-offs

    We help you weigh change orders, value engineering and accelerated schedules against their effect on your renovation's cost, quality and completion.

FF&E and OS&E Procurement & Installation

We treat furniture, fixtures and equipment (FF&E) and operating supplies and equipment (OS&E) as part of delivering your construction, not as a separate purchasing job. We coordinate procurement all the way through move-in and project closeout, and we keep your FF&E spend within the agreed budget.

FF&E and OS&E coordination along the construction critical path.

  1. Specification

    Manage FF&E and OS&E specification as part of the construction critical path.

  2. Shop drawing approval

    Review and approve shop drawings.

  3. Freight

    Coordinate freight with suppliers and your logistics provider.

  4. Storage

    Coordinate storage with suppliers and your logistics provider.

  5. Receiving

    Your on-site staff or a receiving agent you appoint receive and inspect the goods; MotelPilot handles quality control.

  6. Installation

    Coordinate installation and move-in, working with the brand to hold the critical path on schedule.

Engagement Options and Owner Responsibilities

  • Change-order review

    We check every contractor request against scope, price and schedule. Within 3 business days, and before any money moves, we send you a written recommendation to approve, negotiate or reject it.

  • Your spending decisions

    You approve change orders and cost overruns. Any spending outside the approved budget needs your written sign-off.

  • Engagement options

    You can take development, construction support, procurement and IT as standalone services or add them to your hotel management agreement. Without them, full management covers day-to-day operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a renovation of a 40-room motel too small for you, and what project sizes do you usually handle?

No project is too small for us, and a 40-room motel renovation fits within our range. Most of our projects are for motels, economy and select-service hotels, boutiques and resorts of 10 to 200 rooms, and larger hotels get a custom scope.

How long does a typical PIP or full renovation take from the day I hire you to project closeout?

A PIP takes 4 to 8 months from appointment to closeout, with about 2 to 3 months of that in pre-construction. A full renovation takes 9 to 15 months, of which 3 to 5 months is pre-construction, covering design, permits, pricing and procurement.

I already have an architect and a contractor lined up—will you work with them, and can you step into a project that has already started?

Yes. You keep your architect, interior designer, general contractor and consultants, along with your existing contracts, supplier agreements and signed purchase orders, and we recommend new firms only for roles that aren't filled. If the project has started, we review your budget, schedule, open contracts, open change orders and commitments within 10 business days of receiving your documents and give you a written recovery plan, and nothing changes until you approve it.

Are contractor contracts in my name or yours, and who approves change orders and cost overruns?

We act as your agent, not your builder, so you contract directly with contractors and vendors. You approve change orders and cost overruns: within 3 business days of each contractor request, and before any money moves, we send you a written recommendation to approve, negotiate or reject it, and any spending outside the approved budget needs your written sign-off.
