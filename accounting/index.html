Accurate Numbers, Delivered on Time

Our motel accounting and owner reporting services get you accurate financial analytics, on time. Our owner reporting and accounting lead answers for the financial results of every motel and hotel we manage and holds each report to the same standards for accuracy and delivery.

USALI Statements, Variances and Consolidation

Departmental results first

Your monthly P&L comes in USALI departmental format. You see rooms, any food and beverage, and every other operated department on its own before any shared costs are allocated.

Allocated overheads

We also spread shared administration, sales and marketing, utilities and maintenance costs across departments by revenue share or floor area.

Variance explanations

We compare every line with budget and with the prior year. Your monthly and year-end reporting package explains every variance of more than 5%.

Performance comparisons

Your package includes a budget-versus-actual variance report. It also compares occupancy, ADR and RevPAR against budget, last year's results and your competitive set.

Required accounting framework

We work in the framework your lenders and tax filings require: US GAAP, IFRS or the applicable local standard in Canada, France or Australia.

Consolidated ownership reporting

We report by motel, by legal entity and for the consolidated group. On consolidation we eliminate intercompany balances and internal markups, and each property keeps its own P&L.

Ways to Engage Our Accounting Services

  • Switch when needed

    You don't have to wait for fiscal year-end. We take over at any point, starting from your previous accountant's trial balance, general ledger and latest reconciliations.

  • Backlog cleanup

    Before your first full monthly report, we reconcile up to the past 12 months. Our target is to finish that within 30 business days.

  • Named accounting contact

    You deal day to day with a named staff accountant who looks after no more than 8 properties.

  • Questions answered promptly

    Your designated accounting contact answers your accounting questions within one business day.

  • Your data ownership

    Your accounting data is yours, both during our engagement and after it ends.

  • Complete exit handover

    When you leave, you get your full general ledger, vendor files, supporting documents and reports within 10 business days, exported in Excel, CSV and PDF.

  • Standalone service options

    Our standalone services, including accounting and asset management, need no management agreement and no long-term contract. Ongoing leadership of your motel staff requires full management.

Accounting, Payroll and Banking Coverage

  • Daily financial priorities

    Every business day, precise income auditing, receivables collection and cash balancing come first. We check every line item twice before any figures move forward.

  • Finance function coverage

    Our team takes care of treasury, accounts payable, fixed asset accounting, internal audits, guidance on third-party audits and support for your joint ventures.

  • Payroll across roles

    We pay your tipped, hourly and salaried employees and distribute service charges. When staff work across departments, we apply the correct rate for each role.

  • Banking and controls

    Working in your accounts, our remote team handles reduced-fee ACH, bank checks against approved payment records, electronic bank reconciliation, card and payment processing, bank-account setup and upkeep, insurance purchasing support, financial audits and an annual internal control audit.

Daily Income Audit and Reconciliation

Every business day, we reconcile your PMS night audit against your POS totals, card processor settlements, OTA statements and virtual card payouts.

Any amount that doesn't match goes to your motel manager by the next business day. We record room revenue gross and book OTA commissions separately as a distribution expense. Each month, before any OTA invoice is paid, we check both against it.

Tax Filings, Privacy and Payment Compliance

We process and report your lodging, occupancy, sales and use taxes by each local deadline.

Licensed local tax partners submit your returns, payroll filings and other statutory filings, and we oversee their work. Your own accountant keeps your corporate income tax returns, statutory financial statement audits and any filings that need their locally licensed signature. We supply the supporting schedules for that work.

Each month we also export your ledger to QuickBooks, so your accountant can keep using it for year-end and tax work. Our privacy and payment safeguards follow Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR for French properties, the Australian Privacy Act and relevant US state laws.

Card data stays outside our systems because we use PCI DSS-compliant processors. We provide Canadian cloud hosting, and on request we store data for French properties in the EU.

Working Alongside Your Bookkeeper

Your bookkeeper can keep handling daily deposit entry, petty cash or vendor invoice coding, and we take on the rest, including income auditing, payables, payroll processing and month-end close.

We agree the split in writing before we start. For us to close on time, your bookkeeper must post entries to our shared ledger by the 2nd business day after month-end.

Handing Over Your Books

Your handover begins by bringing together the records and access we need. It then takes you through our first close, up to the delivery of your P&L.

From accounting handover to your first P&L.

  1. Provide records and access

    At the start, provide online banking access with limited payment rights; the last 12 months of ledgers, bank statements and tax returns; your vendor list with open invoices; current payroll records; and PMS and POS user access.

  2. First month-end close

    We run our first month-end close for the first full month after handover.

  3. Receive your P&L

    The P&L reaches you within 10 business days of that month-end.

Fees, Sales Taxes and Payment Terms

We quote a fixed monthly fee upfront, based on transaction volume, payroll headcount, properties and legal entities, and functions you hand over.

Non-standard data connections and historical cleanup carry separate one-time fees; third-party software is billed at cost. Under our pricing and payment terms, USD prices exclude sales taxes unless local law requires them. You pay bank transfer charges; we cover CAD, EUR or AUD conversion.

Financial Statements and Owner Reporting

  • Lender-ready financial statements

    Every period, you get a profit and loss statement, a balance sheet and a cash flow statement, all ready for your lenders and investors to review.

  • Reviewed monthly delivery

    After each close, our staff accountants and a licensed CPA review your full P&L. You receive it within 10 business days of month-end. We also reconcile your cash, cash flows, assets and liabilities promptly.

  • International payroll delivery

    We run payroll remotely ourselves for motels and hotels in the United States, France, Canada, Australia and the other countries where we work with owners.

  • Payments and registers

    We handle your vendor invoices, utilities management, loan payments and partner distributions. We also process electronic ACH payments and keep your check registers.

  • Cash planning cadence

    We check your account balances every week and prepare cash flow projections every month. We also support your banking relationships, budgeting, forecasting and cash-flow oversight.

  • Independently examined controls

    The accounting processes our remote team follows in your systems are examined under SOC 1 by an independent service auditor. If you ask, we share our current report under a non-disclosure agreement and tell you whether it's a Type I or Type II report and which period it covers.

  • Access for your auditors

    Your auditor or lender can audit your motel's books at any time. They get read access to the ledgers and supporting records they need.

  • Owner-controlled operating account

    Your operating account stays in your name at your bank, and you keep signing authority. Our limited access covers approved invoices only. One accountant prepares each payment and a second accountant releases it. Any amount above your limit needs your approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we outsource accounting alone for our motel or hotel, or keep tasks with our bookkeeper?

Yes to both: accounting is available as a standalone service with no management agreement and no long-term contract. Your bookkeeper can keep daily deposit entry, petty cash or vendor invoice coding while we take on income auditing, payables, payroll processing and month-end close, with the split agreed in writing before we start.

Can you take over my accounting mid-year, pick up the books from my previous accountant and clean up any backlog or unreconciled accounts?

Yes, you don't have to wait for fiscal year-end: we start from your previous accountant's trial balance, general ledger and latest reconciliations. Before your first full monthly report, we reconcile up to the past 12 months, with a target of finishing within 30 business days.

Which factors change your accounting fee: transaction volume, payroll headcount, property and entity count, systems integration, historical cleanup or the functions we outsource?

Your fixed monthly fee, quoted before you sign, is based on your transaction volume, payroll headcount, number of properties and legal entities, and the functions you hand over to us. Connections with no standard data feed and cleanup of historical records each carry a separate one-time fee, and we quote the cleanup once we've reviewed your books.

In the daily income audit, which sources do you reconcile against each other — PMS night audit, POS, card processor settlements, OTA statements and virtual card payouts — and how are OTA commissions handled?

Every business day, we reconcile your PMS night audit against your POS totals, card processor settlements, OTA statements and virtual card payouts, and any amount that doesn't match goes to your manager by the next business day. We record room revenue gross, book OTA commissions separately as a distribution expense, and check both against each OTA invoice before it's paid.
