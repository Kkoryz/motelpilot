Turnkey Management Across the Whole Investment Cycle

We manage your motel turnkey across the whole investment cycle.

You get a named manager who answers during our business hours, Monday to Friday on Eastern Time. After hours, your on-site team deals with operational issues. We structure our master services agreements to work in your favor as the owner.

We serve independent lodging owners in countries including the United States, France, Canada and Australia. Our owners run motels, hotels, hostels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals.

Services for Your Motel Business

Business Development

Build your portfolio with our motel investment and development support. We assess potential deals, help arrange funding and guide your project through planning, construction and launch.

Digital Marketing

Turn motel searches into direct reservations with our digital marketing service. We connect accurate rates and consistent listings with your booking engine, then track which campaigns bring bookings.

Human Resources

Your team gets practical HR support built around your leadership. We help you hire and support staff, handle payroll and meet employment obligations, with a dedicated contact for your property.

Operations Management

Keep daily motel work aligned with your goals through our remote operations service. We oversee improvements, support your staff and reduce purchasing costs, with help preparing new properties to open.

Revenue Management

Hand off room pricing with our remote revenue management service. We adjust rates each business day, turn expected demand into financial targets and manage distribution around your property’s needs and existing systems.

Information Technology

Whether you need a system change or ongoing help, our motel IT service supports the way you work. We select and deploy technology, protect access and data, and keep systems updated.

Commercial Strategy

Bring your sales and pricing decisions together with our commercial strategy service. We build and run a coordinated plan for reaching guests, winning group business and measuring performance alongside your team or agencies.

Project Management

From a new build to an occupied-motel renovation, we provide motel project oversight that coordinates contractors and purchasing while keeping costs, deadlines and brand requirements in view.

Motel Development

Give your development team a central point of coordination through our remote development support. We manage design changes, brand approvals and furnishing purchases, then prepare the handover to your opening team.

Asset Management

Protect your ownership interests with our hotel asset oversight. We review performance, hold operators accountable and guide revenue and capital spending decisions, including support when your property faces financial distress.

Motel Accounting

Make decisions with reliable financial records through our motel accounting service. We reconcile daily income, explain results in USALI statements and handle financial administration while working alongside your bookkeeper.

OTA Optimization

Strengthen your presence on booking sites with our OTA listing and distribution support. We rebuild listings, monitor visibility each business day and correct rate and inventory issues, independently or alongside your revenue manager.

Questions About Working With Us

Can I hire you just for motel deal sourcing and underwriting, and what do I receive?

Yes, we offer sourcing and underwriting separately, without requiring a management agreement. You receive market analysis, operating budgets, pro formas, an asset plan and a property underwriting model. Fees are confirmed in writing before work, per mandate: a retainer, success fee payable at closing, or both, depending on scope and complexity. You may choose any operator after closing; hiring us to operate requires a separate management agreement.

Can you optimize pricing and inventory while preserving corporate rates, group allotments and distribution commitments?

Yes, we preserve your existing corporate and tour operator contracts, including fixed rates, room allotments and release dates, without requiring renegotiation. We price the remaining inventory around those commitments, release unused allotments for public sale on their release dates and flag contracts worth reviewing at renewal.

Will reports link campaign spend to completed bookings and acquisition cost, not just traffic or revenue?

Yes, our reports connect campaign spend with completed reservations and show the acquisition cost per booking for each channel. We set up tracking in your booking engine to attribute reservations and their booking value to campaigns, rather than reporting only traffic or gross revenue.

Can I keep my QuickBooks and my accountant, or do you move everything to your platform?

You can keep your accountant and use QuickBooks for year-end and tax work, and we handle your daily bookkeeping in the systems we agree with you. We export your ledger to QuickBooks monthly, and your accountant retains full access to ledgers, bank reconciliations and supporting documents. You can hire us for accounting alone.

What keeps reservations and guest operations running during internet, PMS or channel manager outages?

We hold or close OTA inventory through the channel manager or extranets to prevent overbookings, while your front desk uses offline arrivals and room status reports updated nightly. In our business hours, we escalate the outage to your vendors as soon as we see it; outside them, your front desk keeps the fallback going and we pick it up the next business day. Once systems return, we re-sync all reservations, normally within 2 business hours.
