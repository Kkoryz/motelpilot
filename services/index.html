Acquisition, Development and Investment Advisory

Planning before commitment

We prepare your asset and development plan before you sign. We test brand choice, diligence findings and financing together while the deal terms are still negotiable.

Development support

We use our brand and network relationships to support diligence, transactions and sourcing. We stay with you through financing and development, and we have helped partners raise financing and equity.

Investment trade-offs

Lenders and equity partners look for credible, brand-backed plans. Gaps are cheapest to fix during early diligence, but that diligence costs time and money. It also delivers less once you have committed to a brand and financing.

Separate advisory mandate

Our data-led acquisition advisory sources and underwrites hotels separately from our management service. Fees are retainers set for each mandate, success fees paid at closing, or both, and we confirm them before work begins.

After that, the choice of operator is yours.

Transition, Pre-Opening and Licensing Support

Before handover or opening, we review your systems, staffing, contracts and permits, and we handle the licenses your hotel needs.

Fixing gaps before guests arrive is cheaper and faster, but it takes your time and access to your data. If the handover window is short, we complete less of this work.

Our management and pre-opening services need 6 to 8 weeks before handover or 3 to 6 months before opening, and we also give advice earlier, at the design stage.

You choose either a fixed monthly fee based on your room count or a percentage fee of 3% to 5% of total hotel revenue.

On the percentage fee, full management that includes revenue and OTA management is at the upper end of that range, and properties with more than 100 rooms are toward the lower end. The agreed percentage changes only when your services change.

Brand and Franchisor Relations That Work for Owners

We're your single point of contact with the brand, and we push back on standards and initiatives based on what they do to your property's returns.

Renovations, system rollouts and program fees end up as your costs as the owner. Our relationships with brand councils give you a say before those requirements become mandatory. That influence doesn't override your franchise agreement, and it's weaker where our council involvement is lower.

With our brand approval support, we prepare the management-company submissions and join rebranding discussions alongside you. You authorize the franchise agreement, and we hold no approved or preferred franchisor status.

Sales, Marketing and Digital Demand

Coordinated direct demand

We align website development, paid search, photography and video with your guest segments and rate strategy. That makes each one more effective and steers demand toward bookings that cost less than commissionable channels.

Standalone marketing

Marketing comes as part of full management or on its own. When it's standalone, results depend on your operating team delivering the guest experience and pricing that our campaigns promise.

Commercial planning

Our commercial strategy plans bring direct sales, eCommerce, revenue management and digital marketing together for your property.

Booking-level measurement

We track campaign conversions through your booking engine. We report completed reservations, booking value, spend and acquisition cost by channel, rather than traffic alone.

Capital Projects, Design and Construction Oversight

We negotiate each item in your property improvement plan with your brand and agree the scope, order and schedule.

Then we bid out contractors and suppliers before work starts. Agreeing alternatives and phasing up front prevents expensive change orders and long room closures. The negotiation adds preparation time, and some brand standards can't be changed.

Our turnkey capital project management charges a fixed fee for oversight and remote review, and we agree that fee before work begins. Specialist design, engineering and inspections are charged at cost.

We revise the fee only when the approved scope or schedule changes, and we agree any revision with you in writing.

Procurement Backed by Group Buying Power

Group buying power

We pool your purchasing through a group purchasing programme. Its national and regional programs get you better pricing and terms for hotels from economy to luxury.

Purchasing categories

We source OS&E, F&B, hotel technology, equipment, facility services, energy management and other categories through negotiated programs.

Controlled purchasing

We handle orders, approvals and payments on one procure-to-pay platform. We track spend by category in a single owner report, which keeps your purchasing compliant and shows you the savings.

  • Specialist support

    You get category expertise, an account manager, and compliance and risk management. We keep sustainability and ESG in view throughout procurement.

  • Supplier constraints

    Where your brand mandates suppliers or a local contract is binding, that requirement overrides program pricing in the affected categories. Switching vendors takes time.

Performance Reporting, Forecasts and Budgets

  • Forward planning

    We forecast 12 months ahead under conservative, realistic and optimistic scenarios. That shows you the downside risk and how we respond when demand changes.

  • Budget decisions

    We build your budget on the realistic scenario. We use the other two cases to plan staffing, spending and pricing decisions in advance.

  • Market evidence

    We assess demand, seasonality and your competitors, then set seasonal pricing bands and expected occupancy. Forecasts are updated whenever market conditions and comparison data change.

  • Accounting continuity

    With our standalone accounting service, we move your bookkeeping onto our platform and your accountant keeps full access to your documents. Each month we export your ledger to QuickBooks for year-end and tax work.

Property Technology and Systems Integration

Connected property systems

We connect your PMS, channel manager and guest messaging tools so that rates, inventory and guest data match. That cuts overbookings and rate errors and supports reliable business intelligence.

Operational technology

Our IT services include concierge tools and supporting platforms. Moving to our preferred systems involves migration and retraining. Where your brand requires certain systems, changes to them are restricted.

Compatibility and continuity

Our systems integration and outage support needs remote access and daily exports. We replace incompatible systems, hold your OTA inventory during outages and supply offline front-desk records every night.

We also escalate issues to vendors and re-sync reservations within 2 hours of systems being restored.

Lender and Distressed Asset Support

We support nonperforming hotel loans through foreclosure, bankruptcy, workouts and receivership.

In a receivership, the lender pays one flat fee that covers hotel operations and all receivership services, with no separate receiver fee and no recovery percentage.

An out-of-court workout has a fixed diagnostic fee, a monthly retainer and an optional success fee tied to the recovery targets we agree. In both cases, third-party costs are billed to the property at cost.
