Hotel Information Technology, Tailored to the Way You Work

You'll have a dedicated IT representative who gets to know how your hotel runs and helps you choose the technologies that suit it. We don't hand you a standard package. We build your hotel IT solution around what you need.

Choose Your Route: Project, Selection or Managed IT

  • Choose your route

    If your hotel has no in-house IT staff, or runs several connected or custom-integrated systems, ongoing managed IT is the right fit. If you have a capable team or vendor who will take over, a one-off project is the right fit.

  • Agree your plan

    You get a written inventory of your systems and vendor contracts. It comes with assessments of how well each one fits your workflows and what it costs, plus recommendations on which systems to keep, connect or replace and in what order. You agree the plan before we build or change anything.

  • Project or monthly service

    Our fixed-scope projects cover design, build and testing. After handover, your team and vendors operate and maintain what we delivered. Our monthly managed IT plan includes daily monitoring, and your staff report any issues to us.

  • Connect unsupported systems

    If there's no supported connector, we build the links through vendor APIs or scheduled file exchanges. Under managed IT, we monitor, maintain and update those integrations when interfaces change. With a project, your handover includes documentation for your vendor.

  • IT without management

    We're MotelPilot, a third-party hotel and revenue management company. You can buy standalone IT projects, system selection or managed IT from us without signing a management agreement.

  • Our company

    Our Canadian company and 25-person team support independent lodging owners across all types of property, including in the United States, France, Canada and Australia.

  • Remote service delivery

    We deliver every service remotely. Our team runs the work, and none of our staff are stationed at your property.

Remote Rollout, Testing and Switchover

  • Project timing

    Your project takes 6 to 10 weeks from planning to tested handover. We schedule the switchover for a night with low occupancy, keep you offline for no more than 4 hours and put front-desk fallback procedures in place.

  • Local installation support

    You appoint one on-site contact and a local contractor for any cabling or hardware installation. We guide them through each installation step remotely by video call and webinar.

  • Progress and sign-off

    We plan the rollout around your property and regularly review progress and direction with your key on-site managers. We run the acceptance tests ourselves before you sign off.

  • Reservation testing

    Before handover, we create, modify and cancel at least 20 test reservations across your booking engine, channel manager and PMS.

  • Payments and test evidence

    We test card payments and refunds in test mode through your PCI DSS-compliant partner. For each test case, your written report records the expected result, the actual result and any open issues. You accept only once every critical case passes.

  • Data migration checks

    We export future reservations, guest profiles and rate data from your old system and import them into the new one. Then we reconcile record counts and totals and check at least 50 reservations line by line.

  • Switchover rollback

    We keep your old system ready until the reconciliation and acceptance tests pass. After that, it stays available read-only for 30 days. If the cutover fails, we use the tested rollback to restore your original setup within the 4-hour offline window.

  • Managed configuration and faults

    After rollout, we make and log configuration changes on your deployed systems. We also diagnose faults remotely and escalate them to vendors. Your on-site team handles physical tasks such as restarting devices.

Security, Access and Privacy, Covered

  • Managed security controls

    On the hotel devices and accounts covered by managed IT, we maintain multi-factor authentication, role-based permissions and centralised endpoint protection.

  • Vendor security responsibilities

    Security settings inside your PMS, booking engine and other vendor software are each software vendor's responsibility, not ours.

  • Your account approvals

    You approve new user accounts and tell us when staff leave. When you tell us, we remove their access.

  • Our system access

    We use named individual accounts with multi-factor authentication and only the lowest level of access each task needs. We never ask for payment credentials.

  • Access oversight

    We log every session so you can review it, and we check all access rights every 90 days. You can revoke any of our accounts immediately.

  • Privacy and data hosting

    We follow applicable privacy protections and use PCI DSS-compliant partners, so card data never enters our systems. We use Canadian cloud hosting, and French properties can ask for EU storage.

Our Technology Stack and Trusted Partners

  • Partner accountability

    We're your single point of accountability for technology partners. We oversee your service providers, manage how your vendors perform and secure the best contract terms on your behalf.

  • Cloud platforms and services

    We work with leading cloud and web-based platforms. We also work with preferred hospitality technology partners for credit card processing, telecommunications, monitoring, IT service delivery, managed services, and full cyber and privacy compliance programmes.

  • Hotel management technology

    For independent hotels, our hotel management technology brings together our own operating, pricing and reporting tools with assigned specialists, secure collaboration systems, managed connectivity, business applications and high-speed internet support.

  • Commercial technology selection

    We assess and select commercial technology for you, install and configure it, and then train your staff on those systems and their marketing applications and programmes.

Ongoing Support and Updates

Before you sign, you confirm which PCs and network devices your managed plan covers and who handles cloud updates and hardware replacement.

We manage your property's firewall and network security. We also apply operating system and security updates to the covered devices during a monthly maintenance window. Cloud software updates come from each software vendor, and this includes your PMS and channel manager.

Your on-site staff or local contractor replace physical devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do a one-off project and ongoing managed IT differ in later maintenance, the staff time the owner must put in and assurance that the systems stay available?

A fixed-scope project covers design, build and testing, and after handover your team and vendors operate and maintain what we delivered. Our monthly managed IT plan includes daily monitoring, logged configuration changes and remote fault diagnosis, and your staff report any issues to us. Managed IT suits hotels with no in-house IT staff or several connected systems, while a project suits you if a capable team or vendor will take over.

For cabling, network equipment installation or hardware replacement, which on-site people do I need to arrange, and which remote guidance and acceptance work do you handle?

You appoint one on-site contact and a local contractor for any cabling or hardware installation, and your on-site staff or local contractor replace physical devices. We guide them through each installation step remotely by video call and webinar, and we run the acceptance tests ourselves before you sign off.

Your old system stays ready until the reconciliation and acceptance tests pass, and if the cutover fails, we use the tested rollback to restore your original setup within the 4-hour offline window.

Can you build and maintain a custom integration when our hotel systems do not have a supported connector?
