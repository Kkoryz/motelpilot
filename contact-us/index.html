Reach our team in English or French

Use the form below to send us a message. Our enquiry coordinator passes it to the right person on the team, and every enquiry gets a reply within one business day. We speak English and French.

Our hours are 8:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, in your hotel's local time zone.

Find the right contact for your request

  • New property enquiry

    Tell us about your property and the help you need on our request a quote page. It will go to our property enquiries team.

  • Existing client support

    Your usual team contact can help. If an urgent rate or booking issue comes up outside our weekday hours, our on-call revenue manager will get back to you within 2 hours.

  • Careers

    You'll find openings and how to apply on our careers page.

  • Partnerships

    Fill in the form below with the services you offer, the locations you cover and how you'd like to work with us. Our partnerships coordinator will reply.

  • Press

    Send your publication, topic, questions and deadline through the form below. Our press contact will take it from there.
