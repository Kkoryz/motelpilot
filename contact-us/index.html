Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

Every enquiry gets a reply within one business day. Our enquiry coordinator passes your message to the right person on the team, who answers your request, asks for anything that's missing and suggests a call if one would help.

Which languages do you speak, and what are your office hours?

We speak English and French. Our hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday.

What should I include in my message?

Give us your name, email address, the reason you're writing and the language you'd prefer. If it's about a property, add its name, location, lodging type, number of rooms or units and the kind of support you want, but please don't include passwords or guest information.

I'm an existing client; what happens if an urgent rate or booking issue comes up outside your hours?

Send it to your account team, and we pick it up first thing on the next business day. During our weekday hours, your usual team contact can help.
