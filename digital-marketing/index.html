Digital marketing services that bring rate shoppers to your own website

When guests decide where to click, your own booking link is up against OTA offers. Our motel digital marketing builds your online presence on Google, trivago, TripAdvisor and Kayak. We link rate visibility, your booking website and your guest reputation so bookings come straight to you.

Free booking links, Hotel Ads and a live rate feed

Free links first

Before we recommend any paid advertising, we connect and verify your feed. Google's free booking links show your direct rate at no charge, and they start appearing a few days after verification.

Paid campaign readiness

Free links let us check how accurate and competitive your feed is without spending anything. Paid clicks are only worth buying once your feed is accurate and your rate matches or beats the OTAs.

Google paid campaigns

We maintain your metasearch listings. We run paid placements only on Google Hotel Ads, not on trivago, TripAdvisor or Kayak.

Live rate connection

Your booking engine or channel manager has to supply live rates and availability. We connect that feed or check the connection you already have.

Incompatible booking engines

If your engine can't send live rates, we talk with you about routing through a channel manager or switching to a compatible engine before any extra costs come up. You don't need to rebuild your website.

Connectivity charges

On top of your advertising spend, your provider's feed or connectivity charges are billed to your property at cost, with no mark-up from us.

Agency handover

We take over your existing campaigns through a structured transfer of your accounts, listings, advertising accounts, creative assets and performance data.

A Google Business Profile built for motel search filters

  • Ownership first

    We fix any control problems before we start on the profile. If your motel doesn't control the account, you lose any improvements made on it.

  • Transfers and reinstatement

    We request ownership through Google, merge or remove duplicate listings, and appeal suspensions with the required business documents. That way, verification sits under your own Google account.

  • Complete motel details

    We fill in your rooms, amenities, categories, descriptions, and accessibility and sustainability attributes. Missing information keeps a property out of the relevant hotel filters and rankings.

  • One coordinated pass

    We handle content, settings, visuals and trust signals together. That covers the profile signals Google documents for hotel listings, instead of fixing fields one at a time.

  • Photos and activity

    We put your profile photos in a deliberate order, caption every image, and set up questions, answers and profile posts.

  • English and French

    We write your listings and review replies in English or French. If your motel is in France or Quebec, you get French content and replies.

A direct rate that stays accurate, competitive and in parity

  • Competitive direct rates

    We correct your direct rate before you spend anything on metasearch. Then we check OTA parity after setup and again at your 30-day review.

  • Value beyond parity

    If your rate only matches the OTAs or loses to them, a paid click is wasted. So we recommend member rates or perks that give guests better value while staying within your OTA contracts.

  • Revenue issue resolution

    Through revenue management, we alert you to unusual booking pace, ranking drops and parity breaks, and we make the fixes ourselves.

From the metasearch click to a tracked direct booking

Landing page repairs

Before we judge performance, we fix your metasearch landing page so it shows clear rates, direct booking incentives and trust signals.

Booking flow checks

We audit every step up to reservation confirmation and test bookings on mobile. Where a change has to be made inside your booking-engine supplier's system, we give your supplier the exact settings changes in writing.

Consent before tracking

Conversion tracking starts only after a visitor gives consent. We use a consent banner and Google's consent mode, in line with the GDPR.

French data hosting

If your property is in France and you ask for this arrangement, we keep the related tracking data on EU servers.

Booking attribution window

We count a booking-engine reservation when it's completed within 30 days of a click from Google Hotel Ads, free booking links, trivago, TripAdvisor or Kayak.

Verified booking totals

We deduplicate by reservation number, reconcile the totals with your PMS every month, and take out cancelled and refunded reservations. That way, each qualifying booking counts once.

Guest reviews and ratings across every channel

  • Reputation baseline

    We audit your existing reputation, set your response strategy and monitor your ratings across every channel.

  • Every departing guest

    Through a regular feedback collection routine, we invite every departing guest to review their stay.

  • Automated review requests

    If your messaging tool or PMS supports scheduled messages, we use it to send review invitations automatically.

  • Front desk fallback

    If your system can't schedule invitations, we give you a review-request email and link, and your front desk sends it at check-out.

  • Routine review replies

    We draft your Google and TripAdvisor replies from templates and email each one to you for approval. When changes are needed, we revise them with you.

  • Publication under your control

    We publish on the day you approve. Nothing goes live automatically or without your sign-off.

  • Serious complaint escalation

    When a complaint involves safety, hygiene or billing, we flag it to your property contact within 1 business day. We also ask your on-site team for the stay details, what happened and what action was taken.

  • Personal complaint responses

    We never use templates for serious complaints. With your approval, we publish a personal reply within 2 business days of receiving the facts.

Booking.com, Expedia and other OTA listings kept consistent

  • Outsourced OTA management

    Our OTA management includes Booking.com optimisation in 2 to 3 business days, availability checks every business day, extranet closures within 2 business hours of any discrepancy, and overbooking alerts the same business day. You can take it alongside revenue management, either on its own or together with motel management.

  • Shoots tied to changes

    We organise photography when your property opens, renovates, launches a marketing campaign or refreshes its website, not as an unrelated exercise.

  • Photographer coordination

    We run the shoot from start to finish. We write the shot list, negotiate with photographers and brief them.

  • Consistent image sets

    We upload the same on-brand images to your website and your OTA listings, and we follow your brand-specific guidelines on every platform.

Sales, marketing and reputation working as one

Our digital marketing for motels and hotels brings direct sales, revenue management and multichannel advertising together in one plan, with consistent messaging and data-led content.

Our teams agree packages based on your motel's needs and the guests you're targeting. We also offer website development, SEO, online marketing, social media and CRM support. In our commercial strategy, our own sales-trained team works across markets.

It combines sales activity data, targeted e-commerce and cross-selling to bring in direct group, corporate and leisure bookings, and we don't outsource the marketing.

From your first enquiry to your first direct bookings

  1. Send your details

    Send us your website, Google listing and booking engine or channel manager. We review your existing feeds and compare your direct and OTA rates that same day.

  2. Model and audit

    Before you commit, we use your own figures to model the net effect. We also audit your rate accuracy, your parity, your Google, TripAdvisor and trivago profiles, and your landing page.

  3. Resolve and launch

    We fix everything the audit finds before we connect and configure your live implementation, so you don't launch with known gaps.

  4. Review early results

    We aim for your first direct metasearch bookings within the first month, but we can't guarantee them. At your 30-day check-in, we review your visibility, traffic, bookings and the commission you've avoided.

  5. Report net returns

    Your report shows your direct booking revenue after cancellations, advertising costs and booking-engine or payment fees, alongside the OTA commission you've avoided. It doesn't show gross bookings alone.

Monthly bids, feed checks and reporting after day 30

  • Project or monthly engagement

    Your initial setup is a one-time project. After day 30, monthly support keeps your bids and feeds checked when your rates and availability change frequently.

  • Weekly bid adjustments

    If you keep us on monthly, we adjust your metasearch bids every week as part of that ongoing engagement.

  • Daily feed checks

    During monthly support, we check your live rate feed every business day for errors in prices or availability.

  • Monthly profile updates

    Throughout the ongoing engagement, we refresh your Google Business Profile photos, posts and offers every month.

  • Reports by channel

    By the 5th business day of each month, we send you PDF and spreadsheet reports covering impressions, clicks, direct bookings, cost per booking and commission saved.

  • Access and strategy calls

    We go through those figures with you on your monthly strategy call.

  • Alongside your existing agency

    You can hire us for metasearch and reputation while your agency keeps your website, SEO and other advertising. Our scope includes review replies, attribution and the check-in.

Setup fees, monthly fees and an ad spend that stays yours

  • Budget without a minimum

    Paid Hotel Ads have no fixed minimum. We size your budget to your markets and booking goals, and we recommend a campaign only when the expected bookings justify the spend.

  • Campaign management model

    We build the campaign structure and manage your bids and budgets on either a commission-per-stay or a CPC model, whichever suits your margins.

  • Fees by room band

    Setup and monthly fees are charged per property, in bands of up to 20, 21–50, 51–100 and 101–200 rooms. The same bands apply to metasearch-and-reputation-only engagements.

  • Included metasearch channels

    Both fees cover up to 4 metasearch channels: Google, trivago, TripAdvisor and Kayak.

  • Additional monthly charges

    Each extra channel, and any paid Hotel Ads campaign we manage, adds to your monthly service fee.

  • Advertising spend stays yours

    Your advertising accounts stay in your name. Your spend is paid from your Google account, separately from our fee, and it never passes through us.

  • Standalone digital services

    You can buy website development, SEO and paid search on their own, without putting your motel under our management.

  • Website and search billing

    A new website has a fixed project fee. Ongoing search and paid campaigns are billed monthly, with each service's minimum term set out in its proposal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the metasearch and digital marketing fee change with my room count, how many channels I'm on or whether you run paid campaigns?

Yes. Setup and monthly fees are charged per property in room bands of up to 20, 21–50, 51–100 and 101–200 rooms, and both cover up to 4 metasearch channels: Google, trivago, TripAdvisor and Kayak. Adding a channel beyond those, or a paid Hotel Ads campaign that we manage, increases your monthly service fee.

Is the Google Ads spend paid by me directly to Google or through you, and is there a minimum budget before paid Hotel Ads are worth running?

Your advertising accounts stay in your name, and your spend is paid from your Google account, separately from our fee, so it never passes through us. Paid Hotel Ads have no fixed minimum: we size your budget to your markets and booking goals and recommend a campaign only when the expected bookings justify the spend.

Can you help recover or consolidate an existing Google Business Profile if it is controlled by a former agency, duplicated or suspended?

Yes, and we fix those control problems before we start work on the profile. We request ownership through Google, merge or remove duplicate listings and appeal suspensions with the required business documents, so verification sits under your own Google account.

Do I approve review replies before they go live, how fast do you respond, and can you reply in French?

Yes, we draft each Google and TripAdvisor reply and email it to you for approval, then publish on the day you approve, so nothing goes live without your sign-off. Serious complaints get a personal reply rather than a template, published with your approval within 2 business days of receiving the facts. We write replies in English or French, so a motel in France or Quebec gets French replies.
