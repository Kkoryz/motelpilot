Higher rank and more bookings on every OTA you sell on

We aim to bring your Booking.com content score to 100% and leave your existing reviews untouched.

As part of our motel OTA optimization, we refresh your title, description, amenity tags and policies, reorder your main photo and gallery, and configure your booking policies to improve conversion. We calibrate your promotions, Genius, Preferred Partner and Visibility Booster against your yield and review signals.

We make them live with you in your extranet over Zoom. Optimised listings usually climb in Booking.com search within 2 to 4 weeks: 8 of our 12 did in 2025, by weekly position on fixed searches.

Booking.com optimization, on its own or with full management

  • Service options

    You can bring us in for outsourced Booking.com and Expedia management on its own or alongside full management.

  • Brand-controlled settings

    If your property is franchised or soft-branded, we act on the settings your agreement leaves to you. For brand-controlled content and photo changes, we prepare them for approval and represent you with the brand team.

  • Commission negotiations

    We use your booking volume to negotiate your Booking.com and Expedia commissions, review your existing contract terms and assess what each programme is worth to you, including Expedia's Accelerator.

  • Your contract control

    We reduce your dependence on expensive channels and manage channel exits, moving inventory to the channels you keep. Your contracts stay in your name, you sign any changes, and no new commission or term applies until you approve it.

  • Management and onboarding

    Our full management runs independent motels remotely while you keep ownership. Full management and ongoing OTA-only management each carry a one-time onboarding fee, separate from revenue setup, based on your room count, connected channels and systems, and operator takeover.

  • Standalone services

    You can engage individual services, asset management included, without a management agreement or long-term contract. The only thing that requires full management is ongoing leadership of your motel staff.

Expedia listing setup, rebuilds and reinstatement

  • Expedia content and placement

    We aim to bring your Expedia Group content score to 100%, and we actively manage your placement and review signals. If your listing has been neglected, we rebuild its full setup without any work from you.

  • New account ownership

    For a new Expedia or Agoda listing, you complete the partner sign-up, verification and payout details under your ownership entity, so the partner account and contract stay in your name.

  • Listings from scratch

    We write your property and room descriptions and configure your amenities, policies, room types and rates. We set up a new Expedia listing within 10 business days; if Expedia's checks hold it up, we follow up and tell you.

  • Channel connection support

    We guide you through connecting the channel in Cloudbeds, RateGain, D-EDGE or most other channel managers, then take over ongoing management of the listing.

  • Existing account rebuilds

    We rewrite your Expedia or Agoda titles, descriptions, photos, amenities and policies inside your current property account instead of opening a replacement account.

  • Records stay intact

    Because the rebuild stays inside your existing account, your reviews, review score, listing history, room mappings and existing reservations all remain in place.

  • Restricted listing reinstatement

    If your listing is restricted or suspended, we diagnose the cause, fix the content, policy or parity issues, and prepare your authorised Expedia support request. Optimization starts once you're reinstated. Expedia makes that decision, so we can't promise a date.

  • Recovering Booking.com access

    If a former manager controls your extranet, we help you file an ownership verification request with Booking.com to get your admin rights back.

How OTA work is priced by listing and room band

One-time optimisation is priced only by listing count: each property on each channel is one listing, including Google’s free booking links.

Listing condition doesn’t change your price for ongoing management or optimisation, so rebuilding a neglected Expedia or Agoda listing costs the same as optimising a live one. Room types and content languages don’t affect price, and we fix room and rate mappings at no extra charge.

Agoda optimization inside YCS

Inside Agoda’s YCS, we improve your property and room descriptions, your photo set, promotions, secret deals, rate channels and mobile rates. We check whether you’re eligible for Preferred Partner. If your listing has been left untended, we rebuild it from the ground up.

Trip.com promotions, rate plans and instant confirmation

On Trip.com, we set up your promotions and flash sales, structure your rate plans and switch on instant confirmation for you.

Regional, niche and corporate channels

We also cover Airbnb for boutique hotels. You don’t need a minimum number of rooms. A 10-room motel fits into our smallest band, which goes up to 20 rooms. We work with every type of lodging, including hostels, vacation rentals and serviced apartments.

If your property is seasonal, we focus our channel work on the months you’re open.

How OTA work fits our revenue plans

Our revenue management plans each cover one property. Extended adds OTA management on up to 3 channels, Complete up to 6; out-of-plan work is a one-time fee.

  • Existing agreement limits

    Before we change anything, we review your OTA and wholesaler agreements. We then improve your content, visibility and rate plan structure within the rate, parity and inventory limits those agreements set.

  • Restrictions affecting visibility

    When an agreement limits your visibility, we show you what that restriction costs you. You decide whether to renegotiate, and we don't change your contracted terms ourselves.

Daily sync checks, overbookings and parity fixes

  1. Check actual availability

    Every business day, we review your sync logs. We compare the availability and stop-sells in your PMS or channel manager with what each OTA extranet actually shows.

  2. Close exposed inventory

    If closed inventory can still be booked, allocations don't match or mapping errors show up, we close the affected inventory in that extranet within 2 business hours.

  3. Resolve sync faults

    We correct your mappings and keep pressing your channel manager provider until the underlying fault is fixed.

  4. Escalate overbookings

    If an overbooking gets through, we alert your on-site team the same business day so they can relocate the guest. We then settle the booking with the OTA remotely.

  5. Correct wholesale undercutting

    When a wholesaler undercuts your public rates, we review the contract or allotment. We then reprice or restrict it to correct the mismatch.

  6. Narrow promotion eligibility

    When a promotion reaches guests it wasn't meant for, we narrow who qualifies so every channel shows the same terms.

We don't copy one listing everywhere. We tailor your room types, titles, descriptions, amenities, photos and promotions to each platform's ranking algorithm. Working channel by channel, we also handle your rate plans, guest scores, content scores and loyalty programmes.

Daily OTA rank monitoring and adjustments

Every business day, our revenue team checks where you sit in search against your comp set on Booking.com, Expedia and Agoda.

We adjust your rates, content and promotions before a drop in ranking reaches your bookings, and every 7 days we run a full ranking review. We update your channels each business day and work on your visibility signals across Booking.com, Expedia and Google metasearch.

Working alongside your own revenue manager

If you have your own revenue manager, they keep every pricing decision, along with control of your promotions and market strategy.

We look after your listing content, photos, rank tracking and channel visibility. When we propose a rate or promotion change, we send it to them as a written recommendation that sets out its expected effect on your ranking. We don't make those changes ourselves.

How we set comp sets and measure rank

We propose 5 to 8 motels or hotels that guests really do compare with yours, matched on location, rating, room count and price level.

You approve that comp set before we start tracking, and you can change it whenever you like. We measure your rank on that fixed set of searches, for check-in dates 7, 30 and 60 days out, and record your position every week.

We use 1- and 2-night stays for 2 adults, on both desktop and mobile, from your top 3 source markets.

Engagement steps, setup sessions and plan coverage

  1. Find the biggest gap

    Send us your extranet listings and source markets, and we start on the same business day by finding your biggest gap.

  2. Connect existing systems

    We connect to your PMS and OTAs and keep your existing PMS, channel manager and RMS in place, so you don't need to replace anything.

  3. Prioritise channel fixes

    We audit your content score, photos, promotions, rate plans, guest score, reviews, parity and competitive rank, then put the fixes in order of impact.

  4. Schedule live setup

    Setup takes around 5 business days, with one video session for each platform. That's 30 minutes for one listing, 45 minutes for 2 to 4 listings and an hour for 5 to 8.

  5. Approve every change

    We make the content, photo, promotion, rate-plan and programme-eligibility fixes with you on the call, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

  6. Confirm monitoring coverage

    Extended, Complete and ongoing OTA-only management all include OTA rank monitoring every business day. Base monitors your rates and competitors, but its optional one-time listing optimisation doesn't include ongoing rank tracking.

  • Manual channel updates

  • More than three channels

    Beyond 3 channels, we recommend a channel manager that suits your property, then set it up and map it for you. Your property pays its subscription at cost.

  • Your handover records

    After one-time optimisation, you keep the channel scorecard, every setting we changed and all the written content. We deliver them within 5 business days of the 30-day review.

  • Staff training call

    We run a 60-minute call that teaches your staff to maintain your channel content, photos, promotions and rate plans.

  • Access removal

    Once the handover and staff training are done, we remove our access to your accounts.

Measuring results on each channel

  • Before-and-after results

    At 30 days, we compare each channel's review score, conversion, page views, placement and booking share before and after, and we check parity across all your channels.

  • Net booking revenue

    We track your cost of sale on each channel, taking off OTA commissions, promotional discounts, metasearch ad spend and cancellations. We don't judge results on visibility or gross bookings alone.

  • Package profitability

    We work out the net profit on every package we run, so you see whether extra bookings are coming at the expense of your margin.

  • Commitments and limits

    We don't guarantee rankings, bookings or revenue, because demand, competitors and OTA algorithms are outside our control. Here's what we commit to: we complete the agreed fixes during setup, review your agreed targets at 30 days and adjust any channel that misses them.

  • Usable reporting files

    You get your channel reports and handover records as PDFs with spreadsheets, including the underlying figures behind your results.

  • Ongoing reporting coverage

    With optimisation only, reporting ends with the 30-day review and the handover files that follow it. Channel reports on rankings, views, conversion and bookings continue only if you're on an ongoing plan.

What OTA optimization covers and what stays with your team

If you take OTA optimisation only, your front desk handles guest replies, booking amendments and cancellations.

If you take ongoing OTA management, we answer OTA messages before arrival and during stays in our business hours, with your front desk covering the rest of the time, and we process date changes and cancellations within your agreed policy.

You make the call on cancellation disputes, refund requests and chargebacks, and we pass your decision on to the OTA. We mark no-shows and request commission waivers for cancellations and invalid cards.

We also file commission disputes and invalid-card claims in every extranet, including Booking.com and Expedia, within 2 business days after the stay date. Whichever service you choose, check-in, on-site guest matters and relocating overbooked guests stay with your property team.

Frequently Asked Questions

My motel isn't on Expedia or Agoda yet; do you create the listing from scratch and handle the registration?

Yes, we write your property and room descriptions and configure your amenities, policies, room types and rates, and we set up a new Expedia listing within 10 business days; if Expedia's checks hold it up, we follow up and tell you. You complete the partner sign-up, verification and payout details under your ownership entity, so the partner account and contract stay in your name.

I don't use a channel manager; can you still run my OTA listings, and which channel managers do you support?

Yes. Without a channel manager, we update your rates, availability and restrictions by hand every business day on up to 3 OTA channels; beyond 3 channels, we recommend one that suits your property, then set it up and map it for you. We guide you through connecting the channel in Cloudbeds, RateGain, D-EDGE or most other channel managers.

If you rebuild my Expedia or Agoda listing, do I lose my reviews, ranking history or existing reservations?

No. We rewrite your titles, descriptions, photos, amenities and policies inside your current property account instead of opening a replacement, so your reviews, review score, listing history, room mappings and existing reservations all stay in place.

Can you manage listing content and visibility while our revenue manager retains control of rates and promotions?

Yes. Your revenue manager keeps every pricing decision and control of your promotions and market strategy, while we look after listing content, photos, rank tracking and channel visibility. Any rate or promotion change we propose goes to them as a written recommendation, and we don't make it ourselves.

What results should I expect in bookings or rank, and do you guarantee any of it?

Optimised listings usually climb in Booking.com search results within 2 to 4 weeks, as 8 of the 12 we optimised in 2025 did, by average position on a fixed set of searches, recorded weekly. We don't guarantee rankings, bookings or revenue, because demand, competitors and OTA algorithms are outside our control. What we commit to is completing the agreed fixes during setup, reviewing your agreed targets at 30 days and adjusting any channel that misses them.
