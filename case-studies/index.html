How to read these case results

These anonymised case studies show what we changed and how each property's results compared before and after our work began.

Each result compares April–September 2025 with the same months of 2024, so both periods share a season. Owner workload figures compare weekly averages.

Before you sign, we also share anonymised revenue per available room (RevPAR), occupancy and net operating income (NOI) figures comparing the 12 months before we took over with the 12 months after.

We can arrange calls with up to three current owner clients who have agreed to act as references.

Three properties: challenges, actions and results

32-room motel in Oregon, United States

Revenue management.

Rates stayed the same most weeks, making quiet weekdays hard to fill and leaving busy weekends priced too low.

We reviewed booking pace, historical occupancy and local demand remotely, introduced daily rates by room type and arrival date, adjusted minimum stays around busy weekends, and sent weekly performance summaries and pricing priorities.

In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, occupancy (rooms sold ÷ rooms available) rose from 58% to 69%, average daily rate rose 9% and revenue per available room 30%.

46-room hotel in Ontario, Canada

OTA management.

Inconsistent room descriptions and unclear cancellation terms on Booking.com and Expedia left the owner spending hours correcting availability and answering avoidable questions.

We corrected listing details, amenities and policies, reorganised owner-supplied photos, aligned room mapping and availability, monitored listing performance, and handled OTA-related guest messages remotely.

In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, listing conversion (bookings ÷ listing page views, from each extranet) rose from 2.1% to 2.8% on Booking.com and from 1.8% to 2.3% on Expedia.

The owner's OTA administration fell from a weekly average of eight hours to two.

28-room motel in Queensland, Australia

Full remote management.

The owner handled guest messages, pricing and maintenance coordination without consistent handovers, leading to slow replies and missed follow-ups.

We took over reservations, guest messaging and arrival instructions remotely, coordinated cleaning and maintenance with local contractors carrying out all on-site work, managed pricing and OTA availability through a shared operating calendar, and introduced weekly owner reports covering performance, outstanding issues and approval requests.

In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, the median time to a first reply fell from three hours to 24 minutes, counting only guest messages received in our business hours, Monday to Friday on Eastern Time; the owner's on-site team answered the rest.

Maintenance requests resolved within 48 hours rose from 54% to 87%, and the owner's routine operational workload fell from a weekly average of 26 hours to six.

Client results

Revenue management

Daily pricing and closer attention to booking pace helped a 32-room Oregon motel lift occupancy (rooms sold ÷ rooms available) from 58% to 69% in April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024. Revenue per available room rose 30% over the same months.

OTA management

Clearer listings and coordinated availability lifted listing conversion (bookings ÷ listing page views) on Booking.com and Expedia for a 46-room Ontario hotel in April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024. The owner's OTA administration fell from a weekly average of eight hours to two.

Full remote management

A 28-room Queensland motel moved routine operations to MotelPilot's remote team, with local contractors handling on-site work. In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, the median first reply to guest messages received in our business hours, Monday to Friday on Eastern Time, got faster, and the owner's operational workload fell by a weekly average of 20 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What periods are the before-and-after figures in these cases compared over?

Every result compares April–September 2025 with the same months of 2024, so seasonal demand is alike in both periods, and each covers that one property alone. Figures for owner workload compare weekly averages.

Can I see results or speak with current owners before I sign?

Yes. Before you sign, we share anonymised RevPAR, occupancy and NOI figures comparing the 12 months before we took over with the 12 months after. We can also arrange calls with up to three current owner clients who have agreed to act as references.

Which kinds of properties and services do these cases cover?

They cover a 32-room motel in Oregon on revenue management, a 46-room hotel in Ontario on OTA management, and a 28-room motel in Queensland on full remote management. Each case sets out the challenge, what we changed and how the results compared.

How did full remote management work for a motel with none of your staff on site?

For the Queensland motel, we took over reservations, guest messaging and arrival instructions remotely and coordinated cleaning and maintenance with local contractors, who carried out all on-site work. In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, the median first reply to guest messages received in our business hours, Monday to Friday on Eastern Time, fell from three hours to 24 minutes, and the owner's routine operational workload fell from 26 to six hours per week.
