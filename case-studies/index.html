How to read these case results

These anonymised cases show what we changed and how each property's results compared before and after our work began.

Each result states its comparison period: either the same six months a year earlier or a later month against the month before takeover. Owner workload figures compare weekly averages.

Before you sign, we also share anonymised revenue per available room (RevPAR), occupancy and net operating income (NOI) figures comparing the 12 months before we took over with the 12 months after.

We can arrange calls with up to three current owner clients who have agreed to act as references.

Three properties: challenges, actions and results

32-room motel in Oregon, United States

Revenue management.

Rates stayed the same most weeks, making quiet weekdays hard to fill and leaving busy weekends priced too low.

We reviewed booking pace, historical occupancy and local demand remotely, introduced daily rates by room type and arrival date, adjusted minimum stays around busy weekends, and sent weekly performance summaries and pricing priorities.

Over the first six months compared with the same six months a year earlier, occupancy rose from 58% to 69%, average daily rate increased 9%, and revenue per available room increased 30%.

46-room hotel in Ontario, Canada

OTA management.

Inconsistent room descriptions and unclear cancellation terms on Booking.com and Expedia left the owner spending hours correcting availability and answering avoidable questions.

We corrected listing details, amenities and policies, reorganised owner-supplied photos, aligned room mapping and availability, monitored listing performance, and handled OTA-related guest messages remotely.

In month four compared with the month before takeover, Booking.com listing conversion rose from 2.1% to 2.8% and Expedia listing conversion rose from 1.8% to 2.3%.

The owner's OTA administration fell from eight to two hours per week, comparing the weekly average in month four with the month before takeover.

28-room motel in Queensland, Australia

Full remote management.

The owner handled guest messages, pricing and maintenance coordination without consistent handovers, leading to slow replies and missed follow-ups.

We took over reservations, guest messaging and arrival instructions remotely, coordinated cleaning and maintenance with local contractors carrying out all on-site work, managed pricing and OTA availability through a shared operating calendar, and introduced weekly owner reports covering performance, outstanding issues and approval requests.

In month six compared with the month before takeover, median guest-message response time fell from three hours to 24 minutes, and maintenance requests resolved within 48 hours rose from 54% to 87%.

The owner's routine operational workload fell from 26 to six hours per week, comparing the weekly average in month six with the month before takeover.

Client results

Revenue management

Daily pricing and closer attention to booking pace helped a 32-room Oregon motel lift occupancy from 58% to 69%. Revenue per available room increased 30% over six months compared with the same period a year earlier.

OTA management

Clearer listings and coordinated availability improved conversion on Booking.com and Expedia for a 46-room Ontario hotel. The owner's weekly OTA administration fell from eight hours to two.

Full remote management

A 28-room Queensland motel moved routine operations to MotelPilot's remote team, with local contractors handling on-site work. Within six months, guest replies were faster and the owner's operational workload had fallen by 20 hours a week.
