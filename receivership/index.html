Court-Appointed Receivership for Hotels of Every Type

Since 2016, we've completed more than 75 receivership and distressed hotel assignments alongside lenders and special servicers. We step in as court-appointed receiver and turnaround manager across multiple markets for economy, select-service, limited-service and full-service hotels, boutique independents and resorts.

Our work also covers branded and independent properties, condo associations and other hospitality receiverships.

Lenders, Servicers and Owners We Serve

  • Clients we serve

    We support banks and financial institutions holding defaulted or foreclosed hotel loans, special servicers, OREO and special-asset departments, and legal counsel. We also support owners who need a turnaround and borrowers whose lenders require outside help.

  • Where we work

    We take on receivership and REO hotel assignments in the United States, Canada, France and Australia. Where local law requires a licensed receiver, we work alongside that appointee.

  • Before you file

    When you bring us in before the receivership motion, we clear conflicts and help your counsel draft an order that fits how the hotel operates.

  • Defining our authority

    We work with your counsel on the proposed receivership order, which sets out our powers, bond, duties and reporting obligations.

  • Checking our relationships

    Before we accept an appointment, we check the borrower, its principals and affiliates, the lender and the brand against our client and portfolio records, and we disclose every relationship we find.

  • Confirming our independence

    We confirm our independence to the court in writing, by declaration when required. When a conflict can't be cleared, we decline the appointment.

  • Our management team

    MotelPilot provides third-party hotel and revenue management through our 25-person team, which covers hotel operations, revenue management and owner reporting.

Taking Control of the Hotel in the First 72 Hours

  1. Establish responsibility

    We take responsibility for everything, from the initial asset inventory and condition review through employees, vendors, franchise relationships, daily operations and the exit strategy.

  2. Deploy local support

    We don't keep staff at any property. Instead, a local security or property-services agent that we hire is at your hotel within 24 hours of the signed order.

  3. Secure hotel control

    In that same window, we take control of systems, bank accounts and reporting. Under our direction by video, our agent changes locks and safe combinations, counts and seals cash, and secures the front desk.

  4. Complete immediate checks

    In the first 72 hours, we confirm payroll, insurance and utilities, contact the brand, key vendors and staff, and send you our first status report.

  5. Confirm on-site leadership

    If you trust and approve the existing general manager, they stay on as on-site lead. If you don't, we recruit an interim general manager within 7 days, and that person reports to us daily.

  6. Support hotel employees

    Through our human resources services, we help keep the hotel's staff in place and supported through the receivership.

Securing Systems, Records and Licences

  • Recover withheld access

    If the borrower withholds records, systems or cash, we document the refusals for your counsel's turnover application. Meanwhile, we keep the hotel trading through bank, PMS and OTA access that we obtain directly.

  • Rebuild incomplete books

    If the records are incomplete, we reconstruct opening balances from bank statements, PMS reports, tax filings and vendor statements. We flag every gap in our first report to you and the court.

  • Take over logins

    On day one, we take over the PMS, channel manager, OTA and brand extranet logins so that we control the hotel's operating systems.

  • Continue card payments

    We open a merchant account in the receiver's name and switch processing over during a low-occupancy window, so bookings and payments carry on without a break.

  • Begin permit filings

    Within 5 business days, we start transferring or reissuing liquor licences and occupancy and operating permits to the receivership estate. A local licensed permit agent handles the in-person filings for us.

  • Maintain licence continuity

    Under the court order, the existing licences stay in force until the transfer is complete, so they remain effective throughout the changeover.

Honouring Contracts, Reservations and Deposits

  • Review existing contracts

    Where the order allows, we keep equipment leases and service and supply contracts running. Within 30 days, we review each one and decide whether to keep it, renegotiate it or end it.

  • Replace nontransferable agreements

    Some agreements can't transfer or continue, and that includes any prior management agreement. For each of these, we arrange a replacement before it stops. When court approval is required, we get it.

  • Honour existing bookings

    Unless the order says otherwise, we honour reservations, group contracts and advance deposits made before our appointment. This keeps things running without a break for guests, employees, vendors and the hotel's existing business.

  • Handle missing deposits

    If a deposit never reached the hotel's accounts, we still honour the booking. We record the shortfall as a pre-appointment claim for the court and the lender to deal with.

Receiver Bond, Separate Accounts and 13-Week Cash Forecasting

  • Post the bond

    Before we take control, we post the receiver bond the court has ordered. Its premium is an approved expense of the receivership estate.

  • Separate hotel accounts

    We open separate operating and payroll accounts in the receiver's name and hold the signing authority the court grants. The lender gets read-only visibility of every account.

  • Keep obligations current

    We manage the hotel's income so that franchise fees, property taxes and vendor accounts stay current and paid.

  • Restrict historical payments

    We pay pre-appointment payables, tax liens and unpaid occupancy taxes out of hotel cash only when a court order or the lender authorises it.

  • Forecast cash requirements

    In our first week, we build a 13-week cash flow forecast and then update it every week.

  • Identify funding gaps

    Our forecast flags payroll, utility and emergency-repair shortfalls at least 2 weeks before they happen.

  • Plan protective advances

    You plan for protective advances from the start. Don't assume hotel receipts will cover every cost.

  • Request lender funding

    When receipts can't cover a cost, we send its amount, purpose and supporting quotes in writing. The lender then funds a protective advance into the receivership account.

Court and Lender Reporting, Testimony and Fees

  • Initial inventory report

    Within 30 days of our appointment, we report on cash, bank balances, receivables, payables, FF&E, supplies, contracts, licences, permits, the property's condition and any immediate risks.

  • Monthly financial reporting

    You and the court receive our monthly report within 10 business days of each month-end. It includes a USALI P&L, a balance sheet, a cash reconciliation and a budget-to-actual variance.

  • Operating budget approval

    The operating budget takes effect when you give your written approval early on. Once the inventory shows the property's actual condition, we revise it.

  • Named reporting liaison

    Our named liaison gives you asset analysis, revenue and profit forecasts, and updates on significant issues. When required, the liaison gives them to the court as well.

  • Evidence and testimony

    We prepare the declarations, facts and exhibits for applications and testify about hotel operations by video. Your counsel files and argues the applications.

  • In-person court attendance

    When the court requires a witness in the courtroom, our locally licensed appointee attends and uses the evidence we've prepared.

  • Combined service fee

    We charge one flat fee that covers both management and receivership. We submit it with reimbursable expenses in each monthly report for approval by court order.

  • Interim fee payments

    Until approval comes through, we're paid on the interim basis the order permits. If the court reduces an amount, we correct it in the next report.

Protecting the Brand and Franchise Relationship

Early brand contact

We contact the franchisor right away to protect standards and keep the flag. We use our relationships with representatives of major franchises to resolve existing issues quickly, without waiting for default notices.

Written relief request

Within 7 days of our appointment, we ask for a hold on cure deadlines and a deferral of PIP requirements. We back the request with a stabilization plan and a schedule for life-safety and brand-standard work.

Brand approval limits

We can't guarantee the brand will agree. When it does, we document the revised deadlines in a written forbearance or comfort letter and share it with you and the court.

  • Your brand representative

    Through our asset management, we represent you remotely on franchise applications, PIPs, standards and fee audits. Our property evaluations and capital planning support the hotel's value and profitability. This is representation, not approved or preferred operator status with any franchisor.

  • Revenue transition support

    Our commercial strategy gives you a written, prioritised audit of revenue and systems within 10 business days of getting read-only access. For a de-flag or re-flag, we start rebuilding rates, channel mappings and demand 90 days before the change and track bookings until 60 days after it.

Related Services and Our Five-Step Approach

  • Revenue management

  • Operations management

  • Five-step approach

Life-Safety Repairs and Spending Approval Limits

When the operating account can't cover a life-safety repair, we ask you to approve the funding.

We then record the approved advance and report it. Your spending limits keep you in control of capital and over-budget work, and routine operations and emergency safety work still go ahead.

Without your prior approval, we spend only within the receivership budget you've approved and within the limits of the court order. Every capital project needs your written sign-off before it goes ahead, and so does any single item more than 10% over its budget line.

The one exception is emergency work needed to keep guests and staff safe. We report that work within 24 hours.

Life-safety repair funding when the hotel's operating account cannot cover the cost.

  1. Request funding

    We send you a written funding request within 2 business days, including at least 2 contractor quotes, photos and the report of an independent local inspector.

  2. Obtain your approval

    You approve the funding request before we record the advance.

  3. Record and report

    We record the advance in the receivership accounts and in our next report to you and to the court.

Capital Planning and Renovation Oversight

  • Qualified project oversight

    Our project management team is made up of PMP-certified managers with construction experience and carries professional liability insurance. We send you the insurance certificate before you sign.

  • Your contracting authority

    We act as your agent, not as the builder. You sign contracts directly with contractors and vendors. We review the bids and, where needed, select or recommend providers.
