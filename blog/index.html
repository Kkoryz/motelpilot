  • What is hotel revenue management?

    17 November 2026

    Hotel revenue management uses demand and booking data to decide room prices, availability, and booking channels. For an independent motel owner, the aim is to earn more from limited room inventory while checking what remains after booking and servicing costs. Forecasting, pricing, inventory, and distribution work together in this discipline, as explained in Cloudbeds' hotel revenue management guide.

  • What Is a Reasonable Fee Structure for Third-Party Motel Management?

    17 November 2026

    A reasonable motel management fee combines a defined base or fixed charge, affordable minimums, profit incentives, disclosed extras, and workable exit terms. Evaluate the complete structure against cash left for you and the operating responsibilities the manager actually assumes.

    Read more →

  • Motel management models: what can an independent owner choose?

    17 November 2026

    Independent motel owners can run the business themselves, employ a general manager, hire a third-party management company, or lease the property to an operator. Each divides daily work, decision-making, and financial exposure differently; brand affiliation is another choice that can sit alongside the operating arrangement. EHL explains these ownership and operating distinctions.

    Read more →

  • Which Metrics and Benchmarks Should You Use to Judge Your Motel Manager's Performance?

    17 November 2026

    Judge your motel manager on room revenue, profit after fees, service costs, guest experience, and verified property care. Compare those results with consistent prior periods, an agreed budget, and relevant local peers, while separating manager decisions from owner-controlled resources.
