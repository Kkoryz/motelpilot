Practical guides for independent property owners

If you run an independent hotel or motel and want straighter answers on pricing, booking channels and the daily running of the place, this blog is for you.

Each guide covers what to check, which trade-offs actually matter and how to put a change into practice. The people writing it work in revenue, OTAs and operations.

Because all three views sit side by side, you can see how a pricing or distribution decision ripples into the work of running your property.
