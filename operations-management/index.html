Remote Operations Management Built Around Your Hotel

We build a business plan specific to your hotel and carry it out, so your profitability and investment performance improve. Our operations management works as an extension of your ownership team and gives you, as a hotel or resort owner, hands-on professional support.

Ownership-Aligned Business Plans and Hands-On Oversight

  • Ownership-led planning

    Your return targets drive our budget, staffing and capital decisions, not last year's budget. If a target is unrealistic for your market, we flag it during planning because it requires early reforecasting.

  • Whole-property coverage

    We manage your property remotely. That covers budgets, pricing, staffing, costs, contracts and reporting. We have no staff on site, so your teams deliver the on-site services.

  • Local management contact

    If you don't have a resident GM, we handle planning, pricing, budgets, vendors and staff leadership through the on-site lead you nominate. If your property has more than 50 rooms or has food and beverage outlets, we recommend an on-site duty manager on every shift.

  • Existing agreement restrictions

    We keep your SOPs and brand standards, support your department heads and follow your franchise, loan, lease and supply restrictions. Our onboarding review identifies any savings or changes those terms block, and you decide whether to keep or renegotiate those terms.

  • Your business plan

    Your plan includes a 12-month budget, a staffing model, revenue, GOP and NOI targets and a 5-year capital plan. We base it on the last 24 months of your P&L and bookings, on market and competitive-set data and on your return targets.

  • Delivery and review

    We deliver your plan within 20 business days of receiving your data and read-only system access. We review actual results with you on monthly strategy calls. When results diverge from the plan, we update forecasts quarterly, and we rebuild the plan every year.

  • Evidence before signing

    Before you sign, our case studies and owner references give you calls with up to 3 current clients who have consented to speak with you. You also get anonymised RevPAR, occupancy and NOI comparisons covering the 12 months before and the 12 months after we took over.

Group Purchasing Power and Supplier Savings

Our purchasing programmes turn our combined buying volume into supplier savings for your property.

They cover the United States, Canada, France and Australia, and they also cover clients who take only revenue management or OTA management. These programmes also give you category expertise, an account manager, and compliance and risk management, with sustainability and ESG kept in view.

Before you switch, you compare our negotiated prices with your current contracts item by item and move only the categories where the savings are clear. Categories restricted by franchise-approved lists or exclusive local contracts are excluded. Suppliers outside the countries we cover are also excluded.

How Remote Third-Party Operations Work for Your Property

  • Remote daily oversight

    We direct guest service and finances through shared systems, reporting and scheduled calls, and your staff carry out the decisions on site. You get consistent standards and financial control without a resident management team.

  • Operational fit

    Remote management requires a reliable on-site contact and up-to-date systems. It works best for properties that don't have complex daily on-site operations.

  • Management agreement terms

    Under hotel management, you keep ownership and pay either 3% to 5% of revenue or a fixed fee based on your room count. You can also choose to add 10% of GOP above budget. If we miss the RevPAR-index or GOP targets for 2 consecutive quarters, you can exit without a fee on 30 days’ notice.

  • Lead time for a switch

    If you're changing management, bring us in 6 to 8 weeks before the handover date. That window lets us carry out a pre-transition analysis, get logins for your PMS, extranets and channel manager, and go through your vendor contracts.

  • Independent asset oversight

    Our asset management oversees your operations, renovations and operators using read-only PMS and accounting access, weekly operator reports and scheduled video walkthroughs. When needed, we commission independent local inspections, and we review the reports with you within 5 business days.

Your Accounts and How You Can Leave

  • Owner-held accounts

    From day one, every login, account and data record is in your ownership entity's name, so you can leave without needing our consent.

  • Administrative responsibility

    Because you hold the accounts, you handle administrative tasks that a manager would otherwise handle, such as vendor billing contacts and access renewals.

  • Orderly handover

    When our agreement ends, we return your booking, guest and financial records, logins and accounts intact. Your operations keep running while everything transfers to you or your next operator.

Staffing, Recruitment and Payroll Support

  • Recruitment and development

    Our human resources support covers hiring, training and keeping your hotel's staff.

  • Payroll records and corrections

    For payroll processing, you submit department-approved time records and tip, service-charge or pay-rate changes by noon on the second business day after each pay period. We pay undisputed hours on schedule and flag gaps. We correct missing or disputed records in the next run.

On-Demand Operational Services With No Long-Term Contract

  • Start with defined work

    If you're unsure about full management, you can start with a fixed-scope revenue audit, pricing and distribution strategy and team training. These show you our work and its value before you make any ongoing commitment. Any operational help after that addresses the gaps they identify.

  • Limits between engagements

    On-demand help gives you no continuous oversight. Between engagements, problems that need steady daily management come back if nobody manages them.

  • Standalone service choices

    Our standalone services cover operations, sales and marketing, procurement, accounting, OTA management, websites, paid search and asset management, and you need no management agreement or long-term contract. Only ongoing leadership of your hotel staff requires full management.

Pre-Opening Readiness, Licences and Insurance

Your ownership entity holds the operating licences, permits, property insurance and general liability insurance.

We track renewals and prepare the paperwork 60 days before anything expires. You sign, pay, and supply the documents and inspections that only an owner can provide. Because you hold the licences and cover, they stay valid when operators change.

Renewing on time prevents lapses that close outlets or void cover. We carry professional liability and errors and omissions cover, and we add you as an additional insured if you ask.

For pre-opening readiness, you provide the completed building works, the opening-budget funding and your appointed local team or general manager. We help you obtain all required licences, including occupational, liquor and health department licences.

Data-Driven Assessment of Your Current Operations

We assess how your property runs today using benchmarking, reporting and technology for upgrading your systems.

  1. Share operating records

    You give us read-only access to 12 months of P&L statements, PMS reports, payroll and timekeeping records, maintenance and work-order logs, and guest reviews and complaint logs, along with your current vendor contracts.

  2. Rebuild missing baselines

    If your labour, maintenance or guest-service records are incomplete, the assessment still goes ahead. We rebuild baselines from payroll exports, vendor invoices and online reviews. We mark the less precise findings as estimates that show direction, not exact figures.

  3. Assessment fee

    You pay a fixed assessment fee. It rises with your room count, your revenue outlets and the number of systems we connect beyond your PMS and channel manager. Rebuilding missing records also raises the fee.

  4. Extra scope

    Analysing more than 3 years of history costs more, and so does reviewing several properties together. If your hotel has more than 200 rooms, you get a custom quote.

  5. Establish complete tracking

    We don't hold back the assessment until your records are perfect. Instead, we set up tracking while we diagnose, which gives you 90 days of complete data for the follow-up review.

  6. Identify recovery priorities

    Our recovery diagnosis examines your financial health, market position, physical condition and operating performance. We trace revenue, cost, staffing and brand-compliance problems to their causes. Then we define corrections and repositioning opportunities specific to your property.

Tailored Improvements, KPIs and Technology That Drives Results

  • Property-specific improvements

    We use your operational data to design improvements and new revenue streams for your hotel. We benchmark your costs against comparable properties and keep within your capital spending limit.

  • No-capital changes first

    We make labour scheduling changes, procurement savings, rate and channel adjustments, and sales and marketing fixes before any capital projects. That way, the straightforward improvements don't depend on major investment decisions.

  • Capital approval choices

    For each capital item, we show you the cost, the timing and the effect on future cash flow, and you decide whether to approve, defer or drop it. If a deferral lets wear or competitive gaps build up, it raises your costs later.

  • Daily revenue measures

    Every day, we track your rate, occupancy and RevPAR against your competitive set, along with RevPAR index, ADR, booking pace and channel costs. We report these against your approved budget.

  • Operating performance measures

    We also report your GOP margin, departmental cost ratios, labour cost per occupied room, NOI and cash flow against your approved budget.

  • Technology and provider oversight

    Our property-specific IT services cover cloud platforms, payment processing, telecommunications, monitoring, managed services, and cyber and privacy compliance. We also oversee your technology partners, hold your providers accountable and negotiate terms with them.
