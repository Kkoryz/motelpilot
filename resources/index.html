    Decide what to hand over and what to keep

    Not sure which motel tasks to hand over, what to get ready or how you'll keep track afterwards? These practical resources can help. Use them to make sense of unfamiliar terms, plan your handover and agree in advance what you can expect from reporting and rate reviews.

    Four resources to help you plan

    Revenue and OTA glossary

    Pricing measures and online travel agency terms, explained in plain words so you can compare options and ask informed questions.

    Handover checklist

    Everything to get ready before you hand over tasks, including system access, booking records, property policies and supplier documents.

    Sample monthly owner report

    An example of how occupancy, average daily rate, channel performance and outstanding actions can be laid out for you to review.

    Weekly rate-review template

    A worksheet for checking booking pace, availability, local demand and proposed rate changes.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I request one of these resources?

    Contact our team and let us know which resource you'd like. If you have a question at the same time, tell us the term, checklist item or reporting measure you need help with.

    What does the handover checklist cover?

    It lists everything to get ready before you hand over tasks, including system access, booking records, property policies and supplier documents.

    What does the sample monthly owner report show?

    It's an example of how occupancy, average daily rate, channel performance and outstanding actions can be laid out for you to review, so you can agree in advance what to expect from reporting.

    What can I check with the weekly rate-review template?

    It's a worksheet for checking booking pace, availability, local demand and proposed rate changes.
