Development Support Through One Point of Coordination

You deal with your hotel brand through a single point of coordination that covers design, construction management and technical services. Our hotel development support also covers purchasing, handled through a buying group set up specifically for this work.

Brand Submittals and Design Change Control

We handle every brand submittal and approval remotely through your brand’s portal, using the access you give us. Each brand has its own documentation requirements, so we build every package to the current requirements of the brand you’ve selected.

Our change-control process sets out the costs and compliant alternatives before you commit any additional funds.

Budget impact and compliant alternatives before commitment.

  1. Requirement or change arises

    The brand issues a new requirement or someone proposes a design change.

  2. Calculate budget impact

    We calculate the effect on your development budget within 5 business days.

  3. Present compliant alternatives

    We give you at least 2 compliant alternatives before any extra cost is committed.

  4. Submit for brand approval

    If an alternative needs brand approval, we submit it to the brand for you.

A Remote Development Team, From Site to Opening

We serve development projects in the United States, Canada, France and Australia. Our team stays with you from early development through to operations. Under our remote service model, we have no on-site staff at your property.

The same remote team from early development into operations.

  1. Early involvement

    Ideally appoint us before buying land or signing a franchise agreement so we can shape the design and brand terms.

  2. Design and construction

    Our remote team provides brand coordination and procurement during development.

  3. Pre-opening work

    The same team carries development work into pre-opening.

  4. Day-to-day management

    The same team continues into operations, with all services run remotely and no on-site staff.

Flexible Buying Routes and Transparent Fees

Choose brand coordination only, procurement only or full development oversight; none requires a management agreement afterwards.

Your fee is based on scope, the stage at which you appoint us, duration in months and number of purchase packages, not your FF&E budget.

If a submittal needs more than 2 brand revision rounds, or you need accelerated purchasing, we quote that as an addition before work starts.

FF&E Quote Comparison and Value Engineering

When we compare your FF&E and OS&E quotes, we look at delivered value, how well each one meets the specification and how reliable the supplier is, not just the unit price. Our comparison and value-engineering process protects your design quality, and every substitution still needs brand approval.

Compare delivered value and approve substitutes before ordering.

  1. Compare quotations

    Check brand specifications, shop drawings and samples, supplier lead time and delivery record, and total delivered cost including freight, duties and installation.

  2. Value engineer if needed

    Where a quote exceeds budget, propose equivalent products that reduce cost without lowering design quality.

  3. Obtain brand approval

    Put any substitute to the brand for approval before the order is placed.

  4. Place the order

    Order the substitute only after brand approval.

Purchasing File Handover to Your Opening Team

We give your opening team the purchase orders, invoices, receiving records, warranties and supplier contacts.

We also give them a log that shows who is responsible for each open brand or supplier issue and the deadline for it. If another operator is involved, they get the same package and a 60-minute handover call within 10 business days of final delivery.

Design, Construction and Approvals Oversight

  • Drawing reviews

    We check every drawing set against brand standards and your operating plan, and we send you our comments within 5 business days.

  • Architect responsibilities

    Your architect keeps their contract with you. They stay responsible for site inspections and for signing off completed work.

  • Approval preparation

    Within 10 business days of receiving your project documents, we prepare your licensing, permitting, zoning and brand approval applications, drawing packages and timelines remotely.

  • Local representation

    When a filing, hearing or inspection has to be done in person, including for operating and liquor licences, we appoint a local licensed permit agent or planner and direct their work.

  • Company and licensing

    Under our company arrangements, your operating licences and permits stay in your name and on-site work goes to partners or local contractors. Before you sign, we tell you about any local registration requirements. Our Canadian corporate registration details are available on request.

  • Existing project takeover

    If your project is already underway, our project management keeps your appointed team and commitments in place while we review it. Within 10 business days of receiving your documents, we send you a written report on the project's status, its gaps and how to recover. Any change needs your approval.

Procurement, Brand Representation and Standalone Services

  • Purchasing coverage

    We source all of your FF&E, or only the packages you choose, through your suppliers or brand-approved vendors. We represent you with the brand on every purchasing decision.

  • Separate assignments

    You can hire us for deal sourcing, brand selection or pre-opening support on its own, or as the first phase of a management agreement.

  • Imported furnishings

    We appoint and coordinate a licensed customs broker, prepare the commercial invoices, packing lists and certificates of origin, and track customs clearance. For each order, we agree whether your company is the importer of record or the supplier is, under delivered-duty-paid terms.

  • Investment support

    Our business development services look at your investment from an owner's point of view and support it from deal sourcing through to help with valuation and due diligence.

  • Brand representation

    Through asset management, we act for you remotely on franchise applications, PIP negotiations, standards reviews and fee audits. We don't hold approved or preferred operator status.

  • Hotel technology

    We provide turnkey IT project management for your hotel technology projects, including those that are part of a new development.

FF&E Delivery, Lead Times and Quality Control

  • Long-lead tracking

    When you order FF&E, we flag any long-lead items and check their progress against your opening date every week.

  • Substitute submissions

    If an item is late or unavailable, we propose a pre-specified substitute and submit it for brand approval within 5 business days.

  • Opening protection

    If approval or delivery can't meet your opening date, we arrange rental or in-stock items so your opening stays on schedule.

  • Delivery checks

    For every delivery, we check the receiving reports and photos from your on-site team against the approved purchase order.

  • Claims and warranties

    We file damage and shortage claims with suppliers or carriers, arrange replacements and follow every warranty issue through until it's resolved.

Owner Time, Fixed Fees and Independent Advice

  • Your weekly time

    You'll spend about 2 to 4 hours a week. That includes a 30-minute video update each week and the sign-offs we need from you.

  • Decision authority

    You name one contact and set a purchase approval limit. You approve brand submittals, budget changes and any order above that limit, and we handle the correspondence and supplier follow-up.

  • Opening prerequisites

    You provide the completed building works, the funding for the opening budget, and an on-site team or general manager that you appoint.

  • Operating licences

    We help you get every licence you need to open and trade, including occupational, liquor and health department licences.

  • Agreed pricing

    Before you sign, we quote you a fixed fee or a monthly retainer, not a percentage of your pre-opening budget.

  • Direct supplier contracts

    Suppliers contract directly with your ownership entity at their own prices. We don't resell anything, we add no mark-up and we take nothing from what you spend.

  • Independent recommendations

    We don't accept referral fees or commissions from franchisors, lenders or FF&E suppliers. Your fees are the only thing that pays for our advice, so it's based on what fits your hotel.

  • Custom quotations

    For hotels with more than 200 rooms, luxury properties and multi-property groups, we prepare a custom quote.

From Pre-Opening to Day-to-Day Running

Opening programme

We coordinate hiring, training, system setup, sales ramp-up and launch as one programme, adapted to your brand's standards or your independent positioning.

Management preparation

Our hotel management programme covers the core pre-opening work over the 3 to 6 months before you open, with your budget approval and monthly progress reports. After that, for independent hotels, we manage the hotel remotely while you keep ownership.

Daily operations

Our operations management oversees the day-to-day running of your property, covering guest services, the overall guest experience and financial management.

Your hotel team

Our human resources support can staff your new property and train its team before opening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you coordinate with my chosen brand's development and technical services teams and work within its required submission systems?

Yes. We handle every brand submittal and approval remotely through your brand's portal, using the access you give us, and we build each package to the current requirements of the brand you've selected. Through our change-control process, you see the costs and compliant alternatives before committing any additional funds.

Can you work with the architect, contractor and suppliers I already have, or must I use your partners and buying group?

Yes, we work with them. Your architect keeps their contract with you and stays responsible for site inspections and signing off completed work, and we source all of your FF&E, or only the packages you choose, through your suppliers or brand-approved vendors. Contracts are made directly between suppliers and your ownership entity, at the suppliers' own prices.

What do I need to supply and approve during the project, and how much of my own time does it take?

Expect about 2 to 4 hours a week, including a 30-minute weekly video update and the sign-offs we need. You name one contact, set a purchase approval limit and approve brand submittals, budget changes and any order above that limit, while we handle the correspondence and supplier follow-up. For opening, you provide the completed building works, the opening-budget funding and an on-site team or general manager you appoint.

Which development choices change your fee: coordination versus purchasing scope, project stage at appointment, procurement volume, brand revision rounds or accelerated purchasing?

Your fee depends on the scope, the project stage when you appoint us, how many months the work runs and the number of purchase packages, not on the size of your FF&E budget. More than 2 brand revision rounds on a submittal, or accelerated purchasing, is quoted as an addition before work starts.
