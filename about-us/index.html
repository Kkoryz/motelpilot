    A team built around independent owners

    We're a Canadian team of 25, and independent motel and hotel owners work with us remotely.

    Since 2016, our job has been about helping owners earn as much revenue as their properties can bring in. Right now we provide management or revenue management for 60 motels and hotels. Why do it this way? Independent owners should get experienced support without giving up ownership.

    You stay in charge, and we work alongside you, fitting our support to your property.

    How we work with you

    Your property, your accounts

    Your property and its accounts stay yours. To do our work, we use only the access you authorise.

    Remote work in your systems

    Everything happens remotely, inside the systems your property already uses. Our partners or local contractors handle any on-site tasks.

    Services bought separately

    Each service is agreed directly with you. You can buy just the one you need without taking on a wider package.

    Senior roles responsible for your results

    • Revenue Lead

      Our revenue lead has 15 years in lodging and oversees pricing, demand forecasts and inventory decisions, so a senior person looks directly at each property's revenue plan.

    • OTA and Distribution Lead

      After 12 years in lodging, this lead manages Booking.com and Expedia listings, channel availability and rate consistency.

    • Owner Reporting and Accounting Lead

      With 14 years of lodging experience, this lead oversees account reconciliations and monthly reports, explains how your property is performing and points out any discrepancies to you.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who is MotelPilot?

    We're a Canadian team of 25 working remotely with independent motel and hotel owners, and since 2016 our job has been helping owners earn as much revenue as their properties can bring in. Right now, we provide management or revenue management for 60 motels and hotels.

    Do I keep ownership and control of my property when I work with you?

    Yes. Your property and its accounts stay yours, and we use only the access you authorise to do our work. You remain in charge, and we work alongside you with support fitted to your property.

    How do you handle on-site tasks if your team works remotely?

    We do everything remotely, inside the systems your property already uses. Any on-site tasks are handled by our partners or local contractors.

    Who on your team looks after my property's results?

    Three senior leads: our revenue lead, with 15 years in lodging, oversees pricing, demand forecasts and inventory decisions, and our OTA and distribution lead, with 12 years in lodging, manages Booking.com and Expedia listings, channel availability and rate consistency. Our owner reporting and accounting lead, with 14 years of lodging experience, oversees reconciliations and monthly reports and points out any discrepancies to you.

    Talk through what your property needs

    Tell us about your property and where you'd like help. Then we'll go over your priorities and what our team could take on. Contact our team to get the conversation started.
