Our Canadian team of 25 helps you earn more from your property, with remote support that leaves ownership in your hands.

A team built around independent owners

We're a Canadian team of 25, and we work remotely with independent hotel and motel owners.

Since 2016, our job has been helping owners earn as much revenue as their properties can bring in. Right now we provide management or revenue management for 60 hotels and motels. Why do it this way? Independent owners should get experienced support without giving up ownership.

You stay in charge, and we work alongside you, fitting our support to your property.

How we work with you

Your property, your accounts

Your property and its accounts stay yours. To do our work, we use only the access you authorise.

Remote work in your systems

Everything happens remotely, inside the systems your property already uses. Our partners or local contractors handle any on-site tasks.

Services bought separately

Each service is agreed directly with you. You can buy just the one you need without taking on a wider package.

Senior roles responsible for your results

Revenue Lead

Our revenue lead has 15 years in hotels and oversees pricing, demand forecasts and inventory decisions, so a senior person looks directly at each property's revenue plan.

OTA and Distribution Lead

After 12 years in hotels, this lead manages Booking.com and Expedia listings, channel availability and rate consistency.

Owner Reporting and Accounting Lead

With 14 years of hotel experience, this lead oversees account reconciliations and monthly reports, explains how your property is performing and points out any discrepancies to you.
