Choose how much you hand over

Our capabilities let you give us the day-to-day running of your motel or hotel, or bring us in for a single service while you run the property.

Under full remote management, we lead daily operations and you keep ownership. Our team works entirely remotely, so partners or local contractors do any on-site work. Need help with one part of the business? We can take that on separately.

Standalone services don't need a management agreement or a long-term contract. You only need a full management agreement if you want us to lead your motel staff on an ongoing basis.

Five ways we can support your property

Full remote management

We run day-to-day operations remotely under a full management agreement, and that includes leading your motel staff on an ongoing basis. Ownership stays with you, along with decisions about the property's direction, and partners or local contractors handle on-site work.

It's a good fit if you want to step back from daily coordination.

Revenue support on its own

We take care of pricing, demand monitoring and room availability planning. Daily operations, guest service and OTA listing management stay with you. Pick this if you run your property yourself but want specialist help with room revenue.

OTA support on its own

Your Booking.com and Expedia listings, content and channel settings are ours to manage, within the pricing direction you set. Pricing strategy and daily operations remain yours.

It works well if you want to hand over the upkeep of your booking channels without handing over the motel.

Revenue and OTA support together

Here we coordinate pricing, availability and OTA listings so your rates and booking channels work together. You keep daily operations and on-site delivery. Choose this if you want one team handling room sales while you focus on running the property.

Standalone services and one-off projects

Take operations, marketing, procurement, accounting, website or asset management work on its own, or give us a defined piece of work such as a website update, procurement review or annual budget.

Ongoing operations stay in your hands, and you decide how to use the finished work. This makes sense when you have a specific task to complete rather than an ongoing service to hand over.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a management agreement to use just one of your services?

No. A standalone service needs neither a management agreement nor a long-term contract. You only need a full management agreement if you want us to lead your motel staff on an ongoing basis.

What stays with me under full remote management?

Ownership stays with you, along with decisions about the property's direction, while we run day-to-day operations remotely and lead your motel staff. Partners or local contractors handle any on-site work.

What's the difference between revenue support and OTA support?

With revenue support, we take care of pricing, demand monitoring and room availability planning, while daily operations and OTA listing management stay with you. With OTA support, we manage your Booking.com and Expedia listings, content and channel settings within the pricing direction you set, or you can take both together so your rates and booking channels work as one.

Can you take on a one-off project instead of an ongoing service?

Yes. You can give us a defined piece of work, such as a website update, procurement review or annual budget, while ongoing operations stay in your hands. You decide how to use the finished work.

Talk through the right level of support

Tell us which parts you want to keep handling yourself and where you could use help. Then we'll work out with you whether full remote management, a standalone service or a one-off project suits your property.
